Recipe research notes for a soft sandwich bun. Adapted from Serious Eats (Cemita rolls) and Food Wishes (hamburger buns). See also [[Chef John's Hamburger Buns]].

## Dough

| Ingredient | Imperial | Metric | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| All-purpose flour | 12.5 oz | 354g | |
| Heavy cream | 8 oz | 227g | Or milk + 50ml softened butter |
| Eggs | 3 | | Divided (2 in dough, 1 for wash) |
| Instant yeast | 1½ tsp | 7ml | Slightly increased from original |
| Kosher salt | 2 tsp | 10ml | More salt than original |
| Sugar | 3 tbsp | 44ml | A bit more would be good |

## Toppings

- 1 egg + water for eggwash (too much egg wash is an issue — dilute well)
- ½ cup sesame seeds
- 1 tbsp coarse sea salt (better mixed into eggwash than sprinkled on top)

## Method

1. Combine flour, cream, eggs, yeast, salt, and sugar into a sticky dough. (~10 min)
2. Transfer to a bowl, cover, and rise at room temperature until 1½× original size, about 4 hours. Alternatively: proof overnight in a bucket in the fridge for better flavour and texture.
3. Flour dough, transfer to floured work surface. Form into a ball, divide into 6 even pieces with a bench scraper. Form each into a ball, flatten, and transfer to a parchment-lined baking sheet seam-side down.
4. Rest uncovered at room temperature for 1 hour (uncovered gives a better crust than covered in plastic wrap).
5. Preheat to 230°C (450°F) with convection. Brush with eggwash, sprinkle sesame seeds. Bake until deep golden brown, ~17 minutes. Cool on a wire rack.

**Total active time:** ~35 min. Total time with rise: ~4.5 hours (or overnight + 1.5 hours).

**Storage:** Up to 1 week in the refrigerator in a bag. Adding a small ramekin of water to the oven helps develop a thicker crust.
