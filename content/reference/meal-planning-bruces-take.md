I plan a week of meals by selecting proteins and vegetables, stocking my pantry and fridge, and planning base sauces and preparations that can be used in several different ways. I “win the fridge” when I use up all of the perishable ingredients, either in meals, or in foods I can freeze for lazier days.

### An example week for 2-4 people:

1. Pick a few proteins. An example week:

    - 2 whole chickens,
    - 1.36kg/3lbs of ground beef, and
    - 1 roast

2. Stock the pantry and fridge

    - 2-3 Onions, 1-2 heads of garlic garlic, 3-4 shallots, 1 knob of ginger
    - Some sort of salad ingredients
    - 3-4 Robust roastable/steamable veg (broccoli, cauliflower, etc.)
    - 1-2 Starches (rice, potatoes, noodles, bread)
    - Spices and special sauces
    - Oils

I pick 1 or 2 meals each week, and every other meal will be based on the prep done for those. Sunday prep would typically look like:

- Roast chicken (whole or spatcocked)

    - Reserve wings, neck, and backbones for stock
    - Dry brine 3-4 hours and season (in any style you wish; Indian, French, etc.)
    - Roast on sheet pans, to temperature
    - For salads, casseroles, and roast veg plates

- Braise the ground beef

    - Season lightly
    - Let cool and package up in meal-sized portions
    - For meat sauces, taco beef, chili, dressed beans

- Roast or braise the roast

    - Dry brine for 24 hours (often cooking 1-2 days later)
    - Slice and package up for lunches (sandwiches, soups)

- Roast the veg (for 2-3 days at time)
