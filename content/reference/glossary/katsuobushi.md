---
title: Katsuobushi
also: [bonito flakes, bonito]
date: 2026-10-30
---

Skipjack tuna that has been simmered, smoked and dried hard, then shaved into paper-thin flakes. Steeped with [[Kombu|kombu]] it makes [[Dashi|dashi]]; scattered on hot food, the flakes curl and wave in the steam. Usually sold as bonito flakes.
