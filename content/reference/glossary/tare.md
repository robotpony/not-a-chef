---
title: Tare
kind: ingredient
date: 2026-10-30
---

The concentrated seasoning at the bottom of a ramen bowl, usually soy sauce, [[Mirin|mirin]] and sake, sometimes with the [[Braise|braising]] liquid from [[Chashu|chashu]]. A spoonful seasons the plain broth poured over it. See [[Chashu pork]].
