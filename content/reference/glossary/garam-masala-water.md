---
title: Garam masala water
kind: ingredient
date: 2026-10-30
---

A tea of whole [[Garam masala|garam masala]] spices: about a tablespoon of cardamom, cloves, cinnamon and black pepper steeped in 250 ml of just-boiled water for 10 minutes, then strained. It adds a layer of warm, aromatic depth to a curry base without the grit of ground spice. See [[Base curry gravy]].
