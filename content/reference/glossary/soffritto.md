---
title: Soffritto
kind: ingredient
also: [sofrito, sofregit]
date: 2026-10-30
---

Onion, carrot and celery, finely chopped and cooked slowly in olive oil until soft and sweet: the base of many Italian sauces, soups and [[Braise|braises]]. The raw chopped mix is a battuto; French mirepoix is the same three vegetables. Spanish and Caribbean sofrito (Catalan sofregit) is the same idea with garlic, peppers and often tomato in place of the carrot and celery.
