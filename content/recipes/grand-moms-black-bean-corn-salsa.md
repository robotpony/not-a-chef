Classic beany dip for nachos, which is also required for most family gatherings.

## Ingredients:

- 1 small can rinsed black beans
- 2 cups sweet corn
- ½ avocado, diced
- ¼ cup of chopped cilantro
- 1 bunch diced green onion
- Juice of ½ lime

### Optional ingredients:

- 1 clove garlic, via garlic press
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- Zest from lime

## Directions:

Combine ingredients and toss in a bowl or container. Let sit for at least 30 minutes.
