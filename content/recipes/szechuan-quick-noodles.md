## Ingredients

- 85g dried noodles (spaghetti or thin Asian noodles)
- 1 tsp Szechuan peppercorns, toasted and ground
- 2–3 tsp Korean chili flakes (gochugaru)
- 125ml beef stock (beef base preferred)
- 1 tbsp fresh ginger, minced
- 100–200g cabbage, roughly sliced
- 1 tbsp tomato paste
- togarashi, to taste
- soy sauce, sesame oil, rice wine, to taste

## Method

Cook noodles according to package directions. Drain, reserving a splash of cooking water.

In the same pan, add a little oil over medium-high heat. Add ginger and cook until fragrant, 1 minute. Add Szechuan pepper, chili flakes, and tomato paste; stir briefly.

Add stock and cabbage. Simmer until cabbage is tender-crisp and the sauce is slightly reduced, 3–5 minutes.

Add noodles and toss to coat. Season to taste with soy sauce, sesame oil, and rice wine. Add a splash of noodle water if the sauce is too thick.

## To serve

In the pan or a bowl, topped with togarashi.
