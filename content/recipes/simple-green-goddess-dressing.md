I saw a simpler version of green goddess dressing on a wedge salad in Atlanta. It was bright, garlic forward, and herbal (but not emulsified). This is my version of it.

## Ingredients:

- ¾ cup fresh herbs (flat leaf parsley, tarragon, chives)[\[a\]](#cmnt1)
- 200 ml neutral (or nice olive) oil
- 80 ml lemon juice
- 25 ml white vinegar (or white wine)
- 25 ml honey or maple syrup
- 12g or about 2-3 garlic cloves (minced)
- 15 ml Worcestershire sauce
- (optional) 2 whole anchovy fillets or dashi powder/msg[\[b\]](#cmnt2)
- Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper

1. Combine the ingredients in a suitable and sealable container. Add salt to taste.
2. Stow in the refrigerator for at least 6 hours, but 24 is better.
3. Optional: run through a blender until smooth.

This can be made into  a creamy sauce by substituting the oil for mayo or yoghurt.

[\[a\]](#cmnt_ref1)I've tested only with flat leaf parsley (was delicious).

[\[b\]](#cmnt_ref2)I've only tested this with MSG.
