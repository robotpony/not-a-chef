We call this camping cream, but it's equally comforting around Christmas when the weather is miserable.

## Mechanic

Sweetened condensed milk carries both the sweetness and the body — regular milk is too thin and evaporated milk won't thicken the same way. Blend everything **on low speed**; a high-speed blender whips air into the cream and can curdle it as the whiskey's alcohol hits the dairy.

## Ingredients

- 250 ml (1 cup) heavy cream or half-and-half
- 1 can (400 ml/14 oz) sweetened condensed milk
- 3 tbsp chocolate syrup
- 1 tsp instant coffee granules
- 1 tsp pure vanilla extract
- 400 ml (1⅔ cups) Irish whiskey, such as Jameson

## Method

Add the cream, condensed milk, instant coffee, chocolate syrup, and vanilla to a blender. Blend on low speed until combined, about 30 seconds.

With the blender still running on low, slowly pour in the whiskey. Blend a few seconds more, just until smooth.

Taste and adjust — more whiskey, chocolate syrup, or vanilla as needed.

Pour into clean, sterilized glass bottles with tight-fitting lids. Refrigerate; shake well before serving.

## Variations

- **Southern Comfort and Amaretto**, or brandy, or bourbon, in place of the whiskey.
- **Kahlúa** in place of the coffee and chocolate syrup.
- **Fireball** whiskey for a cinnamon version.
- **Cinnamon or pumpkin spice**: ½ tsp spice, and for pumpkin, ½ cup pumpkin purée (not pie filling).
- **Orange**: swap the vanilla for orange extract, add extra chocolate syrup.
- **Dairy-free**: coconut milk and homemade dairy-free sweetened condensed milk in place of the cream and condensed milk.
