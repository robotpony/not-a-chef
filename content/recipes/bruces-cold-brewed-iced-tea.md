This is my lazier iced tea recipe. The cold brew process gets a smoother overall flavour, though takes an extra day or so to get there.

## Ingredients:

- 2L cold tap water
- 6 bags Ceylon black tea
- (optional) 1 bag flavoured tea (peach or raspberry are my favs)
- 100g or ½ cup white sugar
- 80ml or ⅓ cup of lime, lemon juice, or a mix of both

## Directions:

1. Add tea and water to a 2L glass or plastic jug and steep on the counter for 12 hours, or in the refrigerator for 24-48 hours.
2. Remove tea bags and add sugar and citrus juice. Stir. Enjoy over ice.
