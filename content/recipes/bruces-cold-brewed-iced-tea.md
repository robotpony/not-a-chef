This is my lazier iced tea recipe. The cold brew process gets a smoother overall flavour, though it takes an extra day or so to get there.

## Ingredients

- 2 L cold tap water
- 6 bags Ceylon black tea
- 1 bag flavoured tea, peach or raspberry (optional)
- 100 g (½ cup) white sugar
- 80 ml (⅓ cup) lime juice, lemon juice, or a mix

## Method

Put the tea and water in a 2 L glass or plastic jug. Steep in the fridge for 24–48 hours.

Remove the tea bags. Add the sugar and citrus juice and stir until the sugar dissolves. Serve over ice.

## Notes

- Steep in the fridge, not on the counter. Tea left at room temperature for hours is the "sun tea" pattern food safety agencies warn against: lukewarm tea is a good home for bacteria.
