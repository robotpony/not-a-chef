## Ingredients

- 500–700ml cold brewed tea
- 3 heaping tsp sugar
- 3 dashes fresh lime juice
- 2 dashes fresh lemon juice
- ice

## Method

Combine sugar, lime, and lemon in a martini shaker with ice. Add tea and shake well until cold and slightly frothy.

Strain over fresh ice in a tall glass.

## To serve

Straight. The recipe as written is balanced toward tartness; add another half-teaspoon of sugar if you prefer sweeter.

## Variations

**Weight-based batch version:** 350g strong black tea, 40g 1:1 simple syrup, 30g citrus juice (3:1 lime to lemon). Stir over ice in a pitcher rather than shaking. Scales cleanly to any size.

**Cold brew batch version:** 1 litre cold water steeped with tea bags overnight in the fridge. Combine 1L cold brew, 90g white sugar, 65ml lime juice. Stir until the sugar dissolves. Serve over ice. No shaking needed — the cold brew is already smooth and low-bitterness.
