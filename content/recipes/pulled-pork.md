## Mechanic

The pork braises in stock and aromatics only; the barbecue sauce is a separate reduction of vinegar, ketchup, and liquid smoke, not the strained braising liquid. Keeping them apart means the sauce flavour doesn't depend on how much fat and pork juice ends up in the pot.

## Ingredients

- [[recipes/bruces-poultry-pork-spice-rub]], ½–¾ cup per shoulder, adapted with paprika, smoked paprika, ancho chili powder, and cumin
- 1–2 pork shoulders
- 1–2 onions, roughly chopped
- 1 head garlic, peeled
- 125–250 ml chicken stock
- 125 ml cider vinegar
- 125 ml ketchup
- ½ teaspoon liquid smoke
- brown sugar, to taste (sauce)

## Method

1. **Rub:** coat pork shoulders evenly with the adapted spice rub. Refrigerate 12–24 hours.
2. **Braise:** combine chicken stock, onion, and garlic in the pressure cooker pot. Nestle in the rubbed pork.
3. **Cook:** bring to high pressure, then cook 45-60 minutes. Release pressure naturally for 20 minutes. I avoid quick releasing here so that the liquid doesn't get too cloudy.
4. **Sauce:** as the pork depressurizes, simmer cider vinegar, ketchup, and liquid smoke in a saucepan until slightly thickened, 10 to 15 minutes. Season with salt, pepper, and brown sugar to taste.
5. **Finish:** shred the pork, discarding fat. Skim fat from the braising liquid; stir a little into the sauce for extra flavour and body, or discard.

## To serve

Stir some sauce into the shredded pork, serve on buns with the rest on the side.

## Notes

- A 6 L pressure cooker holds 2 boneless shoulders (or 1 bone-in).
