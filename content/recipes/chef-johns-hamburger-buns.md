Based on a recipe from [Fooooood Wishes Dot Com](https://www.allrecipes.com/recipe/233652/homemade-hamburger-buns/). If you didn’t sing that in your head, go find John’s videos on YouTube and watch them until you do.

Even on your first try, these buns are better than what you can find in most markets, mostly because they’re fresh and you have to work for them.

#### Ingredients:

- 2 ½ tsp t(or 1 package) of active, dry yeast
- 450g (1lb, approx 3 ½ cups) of all purpose flour
- 250g (1 cup) warm (40C/101F) water[\[a\]](#cmnt1)
- 1 large egg
- 3 tbsp melted butter[\[b\]](#cmnt2) 
- 3 tbsp white sugar[\[c\]](#cmnt3)
- 1 ¼ tsp salt
- 1 tsp olive oil (non-EVDO)

#### Egg wash/toppings:

- 1 egg, beaten
- 1 tbsp milk
- 1-2 tsp sesame seeds

### Directions:

This is a very standard dough procedure.

1. Add yeast, ½ cup of flour, and warm water to the bowl of a stand mixer. Whisk until smooth. Let stand for 10-15 minutes, or until foamy.
2. Add 1 egg, melted butter, sugar, and salt to the bowl, and whisk until smooth again.
3. Switch to dough hook and slowly combine flour (about 3 cups), until the dough forms a ball. Knead until the dough is cohesive and somewhat sticky (but not too sticky), or about 5 minutes. Sticky is better than dry here.
4. Transfer dough to a lightly floured work surface  and form into a smooth, rough shape. There are many ways to do this, but I prefer to use a bench scraper and pull the dough over itself a number of times, and then invert and use the bench scraper to help form into a smooth-topped and very taught, round ball.
5. Place the dough ball in a greased container, cover, and let rise. If possible, try to rise in a 24C (75F) warming tray, slightly warmed oven, or in a warm spot in your kitchen and let rise until doubled in size. Ideally this takes 1-2 hours. The rise time is based on the viability of the yeast, temperature, etc.
6. Remove dough from the bowl, flatten into a rectangle, and split into 8 equal pieces (about 100g each). Form into small balls, folding dough on itself initially, and then forming into taught balls on the counter with your hands or bench scraper. Add balls to a sheet pan (with parchment or a baking pad), and flatten slightly.
7. Let dough balls rise (45m - 1h) until doubled.
8. Preheat the oven to 190C / 375F.
9. Make egg wash (egg, milk, salt, whisked together), and lightly brush buns. Top with sesame seeds.
10. Bake for 15-18 minutes, or until lightly browned. Let cool completely, and slice with a serrated knife.
