This is a great filling for vegetarian pasta like ravioli, tortellini, or sauced pastas like lasagne or ragù. Makes enough filling for around 50 small stuffed pastas (10 g each).

## Ingredients

- 250 ml ricotta
- 480 g (2 cups) cooked and mashed yam or sweet potato
- 40 g toasted pecans, crushed
- 1 egg yolk
- 25 g (about ½ cup) grated Parmesan, plus more for serving
- 10 g sage, minced
- A splash of lemon juice
- A shot of maple syrup, to balance the sweetness
- 5 g freshly ground black pepper
- 3 g salt

## Method

Bake or steam the yam or sweet potato until soft. Mash and let cool.

Whip the ricotta, Parmesan, and cooled yam together with everything else until smooth. Whipping gives a much better result if you have the time. Taste and adjust the salt, lemon, and maple.

Chill before using. For easy portioning, roll the cooled filling into a log and freeze it, then slice it into 5–10 g pieces to suit the size of your pasta.

## Notes

- The lemon juice was missing from the first batch; adjust it to taste.
