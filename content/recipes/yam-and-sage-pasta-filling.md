This is a great filling for vegetarian pasta like ravioli, tortellini, or sauced pastas like lasagne or ragu. Makes enough filling for around 50 small stuffed pastas (10g each).

#### Ingredients:

- 250ml ricotta cheese
- 60g (2 cups) cooked and mashed yam or sweet potato
- 40g crushed toasted pecans
- 1 egg yolk
- 25g grated parmesan cheese (~ ½ cup) plus more for serving
- 10g minced sage
- Splash of lemon juice ← missing from first batch, play with this
- Shot of maple syrup to balance sweetness
- 5g freshly ground black pepper
- 3g salt (to taste)

## Method:

1. Bake or steam yam or sweet potato and let cool.
2. Whip ricotta, parmesan, and cooled yam thoroughly (with other ingredients).
3. Cool before using as a pasta filling (freezing can make it easy to work with).

If you roll the cooled filling out and freeze, you can slice it up  in 5-10g chunks depending on the size of pasta you plan to fill. Whipping the mixture produces a much better result if you have the time.
