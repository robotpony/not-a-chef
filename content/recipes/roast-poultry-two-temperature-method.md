## Mechanic

Dark and light meat have different target temperatures. Splitting them into separate pans and **starting dark meat 30–45 minutes earlier** solves this without tenting or shielding. No water in the pan: the char marks are deliberate and deglaze into stock. Two temperatures: low to build internal temp evenly, high to finish and brown.

## Method

Season both sides of all pieces with poultry seasoning or your spice rub. Set in roasting pans — dark meat (thighs, legs, wings) in one pan, breasts in another. Rest in the fridge 40 minutes to let the seasoning penetrate. Add a small amount of neutral oil to each pan.

Start dark meat in the oven at **150°C (300°F)** for 50–65% of the total cook time.

Add breasts to a second pan. Raise oven to **218°C (425°F)**. Finish both until dark meat reaches 79–91°C internal and breasts reach 63°C internal.

Rest 10 minutes. Deglaze pans with a little water or stock; use the cooking liquid in stock or gravy.

## Timing examples

| Bird | Low phase | High phase |
|---|---|---|
| Large turkey (5–7 kg) | 50 min | 45 min |
| 2 whole chickens | 30 min | 20 min |

## Notes

- Char marks on the pan bottom are fine; deglaze them rather than avoiding them.
- 2 whole chickens take roughly the same oven space as a large turkey but yield about ⅓ the meat.
- Alternative temperatures used for turkey: 165°C low phase, 205°C high phase — slightly less aggressive browning with comparable results.
