I found this recipe in our old recipe book with the following: All proceeds to charitable projects CHAPTER BF, PEO SISTERHOOD, ABBOTSFORD, BC.

#### Ingredients:

- ½ cups each of 9 types of dried beans (or …)
- 1 ham hock
- 2-4 cups of sausage, turkey, and/or bacon

Soak beans in a large pot with 2x the water and some salt overnight.

#### Aromatics:

- 1 large onion, chopped
- 1 large can of tomatoes or tomato sauce
- 1 tsp chili powder
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 1 lemon, juiced and zested
- 1 tbsp Tabasco
- Salt and pepper to taste

## Directions:

1. Drain beans, rinse, and add to large pot with 2L/quarts of water. Add ham hock and bones and simmer for 2 ½ - 3 hours until beans are almost tender.
2. Remove bones/hock and add aromatics and simmer for 30 minutes, stirring regularly. Taste and season as needed.
