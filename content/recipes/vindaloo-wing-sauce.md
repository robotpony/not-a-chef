## Ingredients

- 3 tablespoons fresh lime juice
- 3 tablespoons maple syrup
- 2 tablespoons soy sauce
- 1½ tablespoons vindaloo spice blend (see vindaloo-paste.md)
- 1–2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
- 2 teaspoons mild chili flakes (Korean gochugaru worked well)
- 2 teaspoons garlic, minced or grated
- 2 teaspoons onion, minced or grated
- 2 teaspoons fresh ginger, minced or grated
- 1 teaspoon cornstarch
- ½ teaspoon granulated garlic
- ½ teaspoon MSG
- salt
- water, to thin

## Method

Whisk all ingredients together. Transfer to a small saucepan over medium heat. Cook until thickened and the oil begins to bubble at the edges, about 3–5 minutes. Add water a tablespoon at a time until the consistency coats a spoon but drizzles off.

Taste and adjust: more lime for acid, more maple for balance, more salt as needed.

## To serve

Toss over freshly baked wings while still hot. Also good on chicken thighs.
