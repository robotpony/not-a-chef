## Mechanic

This is a one-pot pasta: the pasta cooks directly in the soup, and its starch is the only thickener. Reduce the cooking liquid down to just what the soup needs — the released starch turns it smooth and velvety without any flour or cream.

## Ingredients

- 1 package (~550g) prepackaged meatballs, or ground meat
- 100g grated smoked sausage, smoked tempeh, or bacon
- 1 onion, minced
- 1 carrot, diced
- 1 celery stalk, diced
- 1 tablespoon double-concentrated tomato paste
- 1–2 cups dried shiitake mushrooms, broken into bite-sized pieces
- 500ml passata (deseeded tomato sauce)
- 120ml (½ cup) dry white wine, for deglazing
- 180g dried pasta, something hardy (rigatoncini, fusilli, orecchiette)
- balsamic vinegar, to taste
- thyme, dried oregano, salt, and pepper, to taste
- a handful of parsley, chopped, to finish
- leftover tomatoes, zucchini, kale, or other hardy vegetables, chopped (optional)
    - win-the-fridge bonus: whatever needs using up goes in with the mushrooms

## Method

Cover the shiitakes with warm water and let soak while you prep everything else.

Bake the meatballs (or shape and bake ground meat into rough meatballs) until browned, reserving the drippings. Add half the meatballs to the pot with the grated sausage and fry dry over high heat until they start to stick — the sticking is the point, it's where the fond comes from.

Add the onion, carrot, and celery and fry until softened. Stir in the tomato paste and cook until it darkens slightly. Drain the shiitakes, reserving the soaking liquid, and add them to the pot along with any hardy extra vegetables. Set the mushroom soaking liquid aside.

Deglaze with the white wine, scraping up the fond.

Add the passata, the remaining meatballs, the reserved mushroom liquid, and enough water to cover everything generously. Bring to a simmer.

Add the pasta and cook, stirring occasionally, until it's about 80% done — the starch it sheds is what thickens the soup. Season with balsamic, thyme, oregano, salt, and pepper.

Stir in the parsley just before serving.

Hold briefly off the heat to let the pasta finish and the soup thicken further, then serve.

## To serve

Fresh bread, for mopping.

## Notes

- Flexible by design: swap the hardy veg for whatever's about to turn.
