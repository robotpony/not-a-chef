This is a brain dump of my McBurger recipe, for when you’re craving those little, sweet and savoury burgers.

## Ingredients:

- Dempster small buns (3in / 7.6cm)
- Beef (chuck, ground top round, and sirloin, 80/20)

    - 45g / 1.6oz beef patties

- 0.7ml Mustard

- 10ml ketchup
- American cheese (half of a Kraft Deluxe slice)
- Rehydrated onions, or

    - white onions, diced, soaked in water, drained

- Pickles extra sour, or regular non-kosher dills sliced thin

    - Add citric acid (or vinegar?)

- Seasoning

    - 4:2:1 (salt, msg, black pepper)
    - .25 onion powder (optional)

## Method:

- Double grind patties, press, and freeze (or triple grind?)
- Cook at 219C (surface temperature)

    - Top with seasoning

- Assemble (bun, mustard, ketchup, pickles, onions, cheese, patty, bun)
- Wrap in parchment (or burger sheets, or in a pan with a lid to allow burgs to steam)
