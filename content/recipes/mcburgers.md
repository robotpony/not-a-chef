For when you're craving those little, sweet and savoury drive-thru sliders.

## Mechanic

The seasoning is a **4:2:1 ratio** of salt, MSG, and black pepper by weight (add 0.25 parts onion powder if using). Double- or triple-grinding the patties is what gives them that soft, almost pillowy texture rather than a coarse, chewy one.

## Ingredients

- 45g beef, double-ground (80/20 blend of chuck, ground top round, and sirloin)
- 1 small bun (Dempster, 3 in / 7.6 cm)
- 0.7ml mustard
- 10ml (2 tsp) ketchup
- American cheese, half a Kraft Deluxe slice
- Dried minced onion, rehydrated (or fresh white onion, diced, soaked in water, and drained)
- Dill pickles, extra sour, thinly sliced (or regular non-kosher dills, thinly sliced, with citric acid or vinegar added to sour them)
- Seasoning, 4:2:1 salt, MSG, and black pepper by weight, plus 0.25 parts onion powder (optional)

## Method

Double-grind the patties (or triple-grind for an even softer texture), press into shape, and freeze until firm.

Cook on a griddle at 219°C (426°F) surface temperature, topping with seasoning as they cook.

Assemble in order: bottom bun, mustard, ketchup, pickles, onions, cheese, patty, top bun.

Wrap in parchment, or use burger sheets, or cover in a pan with a lid, to steam the burgers slightly and soften the bun.
