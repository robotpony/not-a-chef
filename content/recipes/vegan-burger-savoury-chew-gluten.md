#### Ingredients:

- 1 cup walnuts, diced (and/or cashews)
- 1 cup black beans, diced or mashed
- 1 cup chickpeas, diced or mashed
- 1 diced onion
- 225g (8oz) mushrooms, diced
- 1 cup oats (buzzed/chopped up if you can)
- ½ cup grated beets
- ½ cup vital wheat gluten
- 2 tablespoons soy sauce
- ½ tablespoon cumin
- 2 diced garlic cloves (or garlic powder)
- 1 tablespoon tomato paste
- 1 tablespoon vegan mayonnaise
- 1 teaspoon Worcestershire sauce
- liquid smoke
- Olive oil
- Salt and pepper
