The first best Sangria I’ve ever had was made by my Auntie Diane when Jay and I had visited Calgary during a Junior Roller Derby trip and we were staying with the Pattersons.  This is the way my Auntie Di made it and it’s still one of the recipes I like best.

#### Ingredients:

- 1 Bottle of Red Blend Wine (I’ve used Chaberton Red Blend)
- ½ Bottle of a Zinfandel Red Wine (Barefoot Wines has a Zinfandel)
- ½ cup Brandy
- Fresh fruit: Strawberries, Mango, Blueberries, Orange (chop Strawberries, Mango & Orange into small cubes and remove as much skin from the oranges if possible)

### Directions:

1. Add chopped fruits first (to prevent splashing)
2. Add alcohol
3. If serving with ice, add ice to glass when serving and pour Sangria over ice
4. Use a spoon and add equal portions of fruit to each glass
