Makes 12 - 20 fried cookies.

These are a sort of deep fried donut. Various western European cultures make a variant of these around the holidays, and some call it a New Year’s cookie.

#### Ingredients:

- 3 to 4 cups flour
- 2 eggs
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 cup white sugar
- 1 cup raisins
- 3 small apples (2 large), diced
- 3 heaping tsp baking powder
- Enough milk to make dough stiff (about 2 - 2 ½ cups)
- Sprinkle of Cinnamon (optional); use less than 1 tsp otherwise it’s too overpowering

#### Optional ingredients:

- Canola (or similar) oil for frying
- Icing sugar (to roll in after)

## Directions:

You’ll need a saucier or Dutch oven for frying.

1. Add eggs and dry ingredients to a bowl. Stir in mill until the dough is stiff. Mix only until combined.
2. Heat 4-5 inches of oil in your pot to 160C - 175C (325F - 350F), using a candy or digital thermometer to monitor the heat.
3. Drop in ice-cream scoop sized chunks of dough slowly into the hot oil and use a slotted steel spoon or spider to move the balls around so they don’t stick.
4. Cook until golden brown and toss in a bowl of icing sugar if eating right away.  Otherwise, wait until eating and dip into a bowl of icing sugar.
5. Let cool on a cooling rack.
6. Store air tight; reheat in the oven.  Best to be eaten fresh or within a few days.
