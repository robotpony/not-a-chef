## Mechanic

A quarter pound of beef split into **two thin patties** and stacked on a **three-part bun**. The middle slice of bun is what makes it a double-decker: each patty gets its own deck of sauce and lettuce, so the burger is twice as tall and has twice the crust of one patty the same weight. Keep the patties wider than the bun and as thin as you can handle; they shrink and cook through in a couple of minutes.

## Red relish sauce

- 60 ml (¼ cup) mayonnaise
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) sweet pickle relish
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) ketchup
- 10 ml (2 tsp) water

Stir everything together until smooth. It makes enough for about 4 burgers and keeps in the fridge for a week.

## Burger

- 115 g (¼ lb) ground beef, 80/20, cold
- 1 sesame seed hamburger bun, plus the bottom half of a second bun
- 1 slice American cheese
- 15 g (¼ cup) iceberg lettuce, shredded fine
- 2 tbsp red relish sauce
- Dill pickle chips (optional)
- Kosher salt and black pepper

Split the beef into two 57 g balls. Press each between sheets of parchment into a patty about 1 cm (½ inch) wider than the bun and 5 mm (¼ inch) thick. Chill them flat while the griddle heats.

Trim the crust off the bottom of the extra bun half so it's a slice about 1.5 cm (½ inch) thick, cut on both sides. This is the middle bun.

Heat a griddle or cast iron pan over medium-high. Toast the cut faces of all three bun pieces until golden, about a minute, and set them aside.

Season the patties with salt and pepper and lay them on the griddle. Cook until the edges are brown and the juices bead on top, about 2 minutes. Flip, lay the cheese on one patty, and cook until the cheese melts, about 1 minute more.

Build from the bottom: bottom bun, 1 tbsp sauce, half the lettuce, the cheese patty, the middle bun, 1 tbsp sauce, the rest of the lettuce, pickles if using, the second patty, and the top bun.

## To serve

Right away, with fries and a milkshake.

## Variations

### Mayo and red relish, separate
Spread plain mayonnaise on the bun and spoon a sweeter, chunkier relish over the lettuce: sweet pickle relish with ketchup and a spoonful of chili sauce. The two meet on the burger instead of in a bowl.

### Tartar sauce
Swap the red relish sauce for tartar sauce, on a plain bun with pickles. Sharper and more sour.

## Notes

- No second bun: slice a single tall bun into three. The decks are thinner but it holds.
- A skewer through the middle keeps the stack from leaning while you carry it.
