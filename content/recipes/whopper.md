## Mechanic

A wide, **flame-grilled** patty with every cold topping above it, and **mayo on both buns**: on the bottom bun it keeps the patty's juice out of the bread, and on the top it holds the lettuce and tomato.

## Burger

- 1 large 13 cm (5") sesame seed bun
- 115 g (4 oz) ground beef, 80:20 chuck
- Salt and black pepper
- 2–3 slices vine-ripe tomato
- 3–4 thin white onion rings
- 3 dill pickle chips
- Iceberg lettuce, hand-torn or shredded
- Mayonnaise
- Ketchup

Form the beef into a thin, wide patty, a little wider than the bun, since it shrinks as it cooks. Season both sides with salt and pepper.

Heat a grill to high. Grill the patty until charred underneath, 2–3 minutes. Flip and grill until charred on the second side and cooked through, another 2–3 minutes. Toast the cut sides of the bun on the grill while the patty finishes.

Spread mayo on both halves of the bun. Build from the bottom: bottom bun, patty, pickles, onion, ketchup, tomato, lettuce, top bun.

## To serve

Fries and a shake.
## Notes

- No grill? A ridged grill pan over high heat gives the marks, but not the smoke.
- I don't often grill these any more, rather I griddle them diner style as it's easier. 

## Variations

There were dozens of variations of the burger, but only a few of them stuck with me.

- BBQ sauce (Bull's Eye is the one I remember), and additional onion rings or those fried onions you can buy at the grocery store.
- With mustard, double pickle, double tomato. This was my go-to in my teens.

I've never liked these with cheese. Do what you want, but this one stands strong without.
