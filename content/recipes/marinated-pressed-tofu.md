## Mechanic

Freezing tofu expels water and restructures the interior into a sponge. **Freeze solid for at least 48 hours**, then thaw completely before slicing. The result is chewier and absorbs marinade far more readily than fresh tofu.

## Ingredients

- 400g firm tofu
- 2 tablespoons soy sauce
- 1 tablespoon rice wine (or rice wine vinegar)
- 1 teaspoon sesame oil
- 1 teaspoon fresh ginger, grated

## Method

Freeze the tofu block in its container for 48–72 hours minimum. Thaw in the fridge overnight or at room temperature for 2–4 hours.

Squeeze gently to remove any remaining liquid. Slice into 1.5 cm slabs.

Combine marinade ingredients. Coat tofu and marinate 30 minutes minimum, up to 4 hours.

Bake on a parchment-lined tray at 205°C (400°F) until browned, about 20–25 minutes, flipping halfway. Watch for colour rather than time.

Refrigerate or freeze. Slice for stir-fry or cube for bowls.

## To serve

In rice bowls, stir-fries, or alongside pickled vegetables.

## Notes

- A light coat of neutral oil before baking promotes browning.
- Specific bake time and marinade ratios need another round of testing. [draft]
