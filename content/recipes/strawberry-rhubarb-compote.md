## Ingredients

- 300g fresh strawberries (2 cups), chopped
- 300g fresh rhubarb (2 cups), chopped
- 65g brown sugar (⅓ cup)
- ⅛ teaspoon salt
- 15ml lemon juice (1 tablespoon), from 1 medium lemon

## Method

Combine all ingredients in a medium saucepan over medium heat. Stir to coat the fruit.

Cook, stirring occasionally, until the rhubarb breaks down and the mixture thickens to a loose jam, about 15–20 minutes. The strawberries will stay slightly chunkier; press a few against the side of the pan if you want a smoother result.

Taste and adjust sugar. The compote thickens further as it cools.

## To serve

Warm or cold. Over ice cream, yoghurt, pancakes, or alongside pork. Keeps refrigerated for up to a week.
