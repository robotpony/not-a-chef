For the 2021 Christmas season, we made a variety of soups. This one was one of them, and it combines two of our favourite soups into one: potato-leek, and mushroom-cream. It’s based roughly on [Serious Eat’s](https://www.seriouseats.com/best-potato-leek-soup-recipe) and [John’s](https://www.allrecipes.com/recipe/256108/potato-leek-soup-vichyssoise/) take on the dish.

#### Ingredients:

- 3-4 washed leeks, finely sliced
- 2L veggie stock
- 2 tablespoons unsalted butter
- 3 russet potatoes, peeled and quartered
- 1 bay leaf
- 1 ½ cup cream (or sour cream, or buttermilk, or [cashew cream](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1kJQD1oO2rTovdkK9rq8FGdhJgK1_HLLwp7STzq-aafE/edit#bookmark=id.nyr842kgm4kz))
- ½ teaspoon ground nutmeg
- ½ cup dry sherry
- Salt and pepper to taste

This soup is an emulsion of potato starches and slowly stewed leeks. There are a few ways to do this, but the easiest at home is to blend with an immersion blender, then run the resulting soup through a fine mesh strainer (or a chinois).

1. Slowly cook leeks in butter in a large pot or Dutch oven. Cook on low, stirring frequently to prevent any browning. Cook for 15-25 minutes, softer is better.
2. Deglaze pan with dry sherry.
3. Add stock, potatoes, bay leaf, salt, and pepper. Simmer gently, and cook until the potatoes start to fall apart.
4. Discard bay leaf.
5. Blend until smooth, taking care not to over blend. Strain soup through a fine mesh strainer, and continue heating on low.
6. Add cream and nutmeg, and taste for seasoning.

#### Mushroom garnish (optional):

- 1 bag of mushrooms, washed and sliced
- 1 clove garlic
- 2 teaspoons of thyme
- Salt/pepper to taste

We like to add sautéd mushrooms as a pop of savoury and colour.

1. Melt the butter and add the mushrooms to a pan on medium heat, stirring constantly. Once the mushrooms expel their water, add the garlic, thyme, and season. Add more butter if the pan is too dry.
2. Sauté until browned and reserve for topping.
