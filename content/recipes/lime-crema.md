## Ingredients

- 125ml Mexican crema, sour cream, or Greek yoghurt
- 30ml mayonnaise
- 30ml fresh lime juice (~1 lime)
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- ½ tsp kosher salt (adjust to taste)
- 30g fresh cilantro (½ cup loosely packed), finely chopped
- ¼ tsp onion powder (or dehydrated or minced fresh onion)
- 1–2g MSG

## Method

Whisk or stir all ingredients together until combined. Taste and adjust salt and lime.

Refrigerate for 15 minutes before serving if time allows; the flavours settle.

## To serve

On tacos, fajita bowls, fish, roasted vegetables, or as a dipping sauce. Keeps refrigerated for 3 days.
