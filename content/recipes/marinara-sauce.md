A basic marinara sauce can be used directly or as a base to other sauces. This recipe is based on [Chef John’s](https://www.allrecipes.com/recipe/235565/chef-johns-tomato-sauce/) and others I have seen. It’s thicker and richer than a [pizza sauce](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1S5WIHwjKUhwJk81cAxMp_hggQVWdj2ytANTs367LQxM/edit), and can be adapted as a base for savoury sauces (meat or mushroom), or into other recipes (like pastas, soups, and stews).

#### Main ingredients:

- 1 (820ml/28oz) tin of San Marzano tomatoes
- 1 (410ml/14oz) small tin diced fire roasted tomatoes (optional)
- 1 ½ tablespoons of tomato paste
- 4 cloves of garlic, finely minced
- 1 white or yellow onion finely minced
- 1 teaspoon oregano (or italian herbs)
- 2 teaspoon red pepper flakes (optional)
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Olive oil
- Butter (to finish, also optional)

### Directions:

1. Shred onions and garlic and add to a pan on medium/lop with some olive oil. Cook without browning until soft. Add tomato paste and stir in, cooking for 1-2 minutes without browning. Optionally add white wine, stock, or water and clean up the pan (which shouldn’t have much brown on it).
2. Crush tomatoes by hand or with a pastry cutter, and add to onion base. Simmer on medium/low.
3. Add salt, pepper, and (optionally) sugar to taste. Add half of the herbs.
4. Simmer for 1-2 hours, adding water/stock to maintain a smooth consistency.
5. Add remaining herbs, and taste for seasoning (adjusting as needed).

If you use good quality tomatoes the result should not be bitter.
