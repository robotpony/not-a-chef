## Mechanic

Yogurt is the key acid and tenderizer — it produces the slight tang and soft interior that sets naan apart from other flatbreads. **Portioning into 100g balls and cold-proofing overnight** gives the dough time to develop flavour and makes the bread easier to roll and cook.

## Ingredients

- 620g bread flour
- 7g instant dry yeast (2 tsp)
- 12g kosher salt (2½ tsp)
- 22g sugar (~2 tbsp)
- 400g full-fat yogurt or whole milk
- 110g unsalted butter, melted

## Method

Combine flour, yeast, salt, and sugar. Add yogurt and melted butter; mix until a shaggy dough forms. Knead by hand or stand mixer for 8–10 minutes until smooth and elastic. The dough should be soft but not sticky.

Cover and let rise at room temperature until doubled, about 1–1.5 hours.

Divide into 100g portions. Shape into tight balls and place on a floured tray. Refrigerate overnight, uncovered or loosely covered.

Pull from the fridge 30 minutes before cooking. On a lightly floured surface, roll each ball into a rough oval, about 5mm thick.

Cook in a very hot dry pan (cast iron or similar) or over a grill. The pan should be smoking. Cook until large bubbles form and the underside is charred in spots, 1–2 minutes. Flip and cook another minute.

Brush immediately with melted butter. Add garlic and fresh herbs if using.

## To serve

Warm, alongside curries or dips. Stack in a towel to keep soft.

## Notes

- Cold proof is important; room-temp-only proofed dough works but is harder to handle and less flavourful.
- Whole milk can substitute for yogurt; the result is slightly less tangy.
