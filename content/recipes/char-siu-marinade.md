## Mechanic

A **ratio-based marinade** where "1" equals ¼ cup (60ml). Doubling or tripling scales cleanly. The marinade finishes as a reduction glaze — simmer the remainder until thick and sticky while the meat rests.

## Ingredients (ratio: 1 = 60ml / ¼ cup)

- 1 part soy sauce
- 1 part oyster sauce
- 1 part sweet soy sauce (or hoisin in a pinch)
- 1 part honey (or maple syrup)
- 2 parts brown sugar
- ⅓ part five-spice powder
- ⅙ part white pepper
- 1 part ginger-garlic paste
- ½ part Shaoxing rice wine
- ⅓ part red food colouring (optional)

## Method

Whisk all ingredients together. Coat meat thoroughly.

Marinate in the fridge for 2–24 hours. Longer is better for bone-in pieces.

Roast at 200°C (400°F) until 65°C internal for white meat, 85°C for dark meat. Turn pieces if browning too quickly.

Pour remaining marinade into a small saucepan and simmer until thick, glossy, and reduced by about half. Brush over meat in the last few minutes of cooking or use as a dipping sauce.

## To serve

Over jasmine rice with stir-fried greens. Also good sliced over noodles.

## Notes

- Tested on small boneless chicken thighs. Good result.
- Without food colouring, the meat is brown rather than the classic red; no flavour difference.
