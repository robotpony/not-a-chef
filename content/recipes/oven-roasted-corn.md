This recipe works for most vegetables, though corn is a slightly special case as it needs a bit longer than most veg. It sounds simple (and is), but the trick is to get the corn to brown and char a bit, for those summer roasting flavours.

## Ingredients

- 4+ cobs fresh corn, shucked
- olive oil (not extra-virgin), butter, or neutral oil
- salt and pepper

## Method

Toss the corn with oil or spread butter evenly over it. Season with salt and pepper and let sit for 20–60 minutes.

Heat the oven to 220°C (425°F). Roast until browned and charred in spots, about 30 minutes, turning halfway through.
