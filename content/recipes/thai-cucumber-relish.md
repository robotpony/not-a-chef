## Ingredients

- 1 cucumber, peeled, seeded, and cut into ½ cm dice
- 60ml white vinegar (¼ cup)
- 2 tbsp sugar
- ¼ tsp red pepper flakes
- ¼ cup fresh mint leaves, coarsely chopped
- 1 shallot, minced
- 1 tbsp fish sauce

## Method

Stir together vinegar and sugar until dissolved. Add fish sauce, red pepper flakes, shallot, and cucumber. Toss to combine. Fold in mint just before serving.

## To serve

Alongside satay, fried foods, grilled meat, or rice paper rolls. Use within a few hours; the mint wilts and the cucumber softens overnight.
