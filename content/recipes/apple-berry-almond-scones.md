First made by Bruce for Ann on Mother’s Day 💜

#### Ingredients:

- 650g Flour
- 100g Caster (Bakers) Sugar; if you do not have access to Caster Sugar, White Sugar can be ground down slightly with a Mortar and Pestle
- 20g Baking Powder
- 1 tsp Cinnamon
- Pinch of Salt
- 125g Butter, cold and diced
- 1 Apple, large (or 2 medium) peeled and diced
- 2 Eggs
- 200ml Buttermilk
- 2 tsp Vanilla
- Handful of fresh Berries (Raspberries, Blueberries, or Black Berries)
- Handful of flaked Almonds
- Icing Sugar, to dues

### Directions:

1. Preheat the oven to 200 degrees C (180 C fan assisted).
2. In a large bowl combine the Flour, Sugar, Baking Powder, Salt, and Cinnamon.
3. Rub the diced butter into the flour using your fingertips until the mix resembles breadcrumbs in texture.
4. Add and mix the diced Apple to the flour mixture.
5. Add the Eggs, Buttermilk, and Vanilla and combine all the ingredients together to form a rough dough.
6. Turn the scone mixture out onto a lightly floured work surface and knead gently until all the flour has been combined.
7. Using a rolling pin to roll out the scone mixture into about 1-inch thickness.  Cut the scones, use a floured cutter and place onto a baking tray lined with parchment paper evenly spaced apart.
8. Make an indentation into the centre of each scone using your thumb.  Brush the top of each scone with a beaten egg.  Stuff the indentation in each scone with 1 to 2 fresh berries of your choice and top with flaked almonds.
9. Place scones in the preheated oven and bake for approximately 16 to 18 minutes.  Half-way through baking, rotate the baking tray in the oven to ensure an even bake.
10. Once baked, allow to cool on a wire rack.  Then dust with icing sugar.
