## Ingredients

- 430g canned chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- 140g cooked beets
- 2 tsp dried or fresh oregano
- 30g extra virgin olive oil (2 tbsp)
- ½ tsp minced garlic
- 1 medium lemon, zest finely grated and juiced
- black pepper
- salt

## Method

Blend all ingredients until smooth. Taste and adjust salt, lemon, and garlic.

If the texture is too thick, add water or more olive oil a tablespoon at a time. The dip thickens in the fridge.

## To serve

With pita, flatbread, raw vegetables, or as part of a mezze spread. A drizzle of olive oil and a pinch of oregano on top.

## Notes

- Rinsing the chickpeas is optional; not rinsing gives a slightly beany flavour that works here.
- Roasted beets are more intensely flavoured than boiled; canned beets are convenient and consistent.
