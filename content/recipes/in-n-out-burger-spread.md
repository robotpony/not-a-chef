## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup mayonnaise
- 3 tbsp ketchup
- 1/2 tsp yellow mustard
- 1 tbsp white vinegar
- 2 tsp sweet pickle relish
- 2 tsp sugar
- 2 tsp finely minced white onion
- 1/4 tsp paprika
- 0.1 g MSG (about 1/32 tsp, a smidgen; 0.05% of total weight)
- Salt and pepper, to taste

## Method

Combine the mayonnaise, ketchup, mustard, vinegar, relish, sugar, onion, paprika, and MSG in a mixing bowl. Stir until uniform in colour with no streaks of ketchup or mustard visible.

Taste and adjust with salt and pepper.

Cover and refrigerate at least 1 hour, until the onion and pickle flavours have mellowed into the base.

## To serve

Spread on burger buns, use as a sandwich spread, or serve as a dip for fries.

## Notes

- 0.05% MSG by weight is small enough that a kitchen scale is the only reliable way to measure it; volume spoons this small (smidgen, drop) are approximate.
- Keeps refrigerated about 1 week in a sealed container.
