### Directions:

1. Season beef with paprika, salt, and pepper (optionally adding some flour for extra browning), and brown with some oil in a hot skillet. Remove beef and reserve in a bowl.
2. In the same skillet, brown mushrooms, adding more oil. As they release their water, scrape the pan, and continue cooking until they start to brown. Add optional shallots, onions, and optional garlic, mustard, and cook for a few more minutes.
3. Deglaze the pan with the cooking wine (or sherry, or vinegar). Add half of the broth and reduce slightly.
4. Add remaining broth and simmer:

    1. Add reserved hamburger and simmer for 30 minutes, or
    2. Add reserved chuck slices and simmer for ~1 hour (or until beef is tender), or
    3. Simmer for 20 minutes, then add sliced tenderloin when serving.

5. Cook egg noodles to al-dente per package instructions.
6. Add sour cream or creme fraiche. When using sour cream, temper it by adding hot liquid in small batches to a bowl of sour cream, and take care not to overheat or it will  break.
7. Stir in chives, parsley, and/or fresh thyme leaves. Salt and pepper to taste.
8. Serve over noodles and top with extra sour cream if desired.

You can speed up simmer time by using a pressure cooker (10 minutes for ground beef, and 20 minutes for chuck) in a standard stove top model.
