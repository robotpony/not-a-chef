This is a simplified satay marinade, great for chicken, tofu, pork, and beef. This is based loosely on [Chef John’s Penang Pork Satay](https://www.allrecipes.com/recipe/275397/penang-pork-satay/).

#### Ingredients:

- 2 tsp turmeric
- 1 small (4cm) piece of fresh ginger (grated or sliced)
- 1 large shallot, diced
- 8 cloves garlic, diced
- 3 tbsp ancho chili powder
- 2 tsp ground coriander
- 1 tsp chipotle (or paprika)
- ¼ cup brown sugar
- ¼ cup rice vinegar
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp tamarind paste
- 1 lime, juiced and zest
- ½ bunch of packed cilantro (optional)
- 1 tbsp fish sauce or worcestershire (optional)
- 1 tbsp of any fairly neutral oil

#### Protein:

- 1 tbsp salt (to dry brine + season protein)
- Protein (chicken thighs, pork shoulder, chuck roast, tofu, mushrooms, etc.)

### Directions:

1. Combine everything but the salt in a blender or food processor. Blend until smoothish.
2. Add marinade to protein and work in thoroughly (1-2 minutes). Refrigerate for up to 4 hours.
3. Cook protein until done:

    1. 230C/450F oven on a foil lined pan
    2. Fry on med/high
    3. Grill on high, turning every 2-3 minutes
