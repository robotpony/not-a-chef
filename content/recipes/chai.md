## Mechanic

This breaks English tea rules on purpose. Boiling the tea with spices for 5 minutes and then **"boiling over" the milk three times** produces a different flavour than standard steep-and-strain: more caramelized, less grassy, with the spices fully integrated. The goal colour is caramel sauce.

## Ingredients

- 500–750ml water
- 3–4 whole cloves
- 3–4 cardamom pods, lightly crushed
- a few slices fresh ginger
- 2 tsp loose-leaf black tea (Scottish breakfast or similar strong black)
- 2 tsp sugar
- 120ml whole milk or 60ml cream

## Method

Combine water, cloves, cardamom, and ginger in a small saucepan. Bring to a boil. Simmer 2–3 minutes. Toast spices in the pan first if you want a deeper flavour.

Add tea and simmer for 5 minutes.

Add milk or cream. Bring to a boil — the moment it foams and rises, remove from heat and stir back down. Repeat three times. This is the "boil over" technique; watch it closely.

Strain and serve.

## To serve

In small cups. Add more sugar at the table.

## Notes

- Scottish breakfast tea is the go-to; strong enough to stand up to the spice and milk.
- Cinnamon stick is a common addition; not essential with clove and cardamom already present.
- Chai masala blends can be used in place of whole spices.
