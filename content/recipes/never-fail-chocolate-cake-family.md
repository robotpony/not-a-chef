#### Ingredients:

- 2 cups flour
- 2 cups sugar
- 4 eggs, unbeaten
- 1 cup boiling water
- ⅔ cup sour milk
- 9 tablespoons cocoa
- ½ cup butter
- 2 teaspoons soda
- 2 teaspoons vanilla

Sour milk:

- ½ cup evaporated milk
- ½ cup cold water
- (Or, 1 cup whole milk)
- 1 tablespoon vinegar or lemon juice

### Method:

1. Cream butter and sugar, add eggs.
2. Mix the milk, soda and vanilla. Add to egg mixture, mix well. Add cocoa and water, mix well.
3. Add flour, mix well. Batter will be very thin.
4. Pour into a greased and floured 9x13 (medium) pan. Bake at 175 C / 350 F for 45 minutes.
