One day, Dad and I had a craving for Crème Brûlée and not having anywhere to go to have it, we decided to make it at home. I found a recipe online and it’s worked great for us each time; including birthday parties and friends over for dinner. The recipe I use is from Ricardo Cuisine: https://www.ricardocuisine.com/en/recipes/4016-creme-brulee

#### Ingredients:

- 2 1/4 cups (560 ml) 35% cream
- 1 vanilla bean or 1 tsp Pure Vanilla Extract
- 5 egg yolks
- 1/4 cup (60 ml) sugar
- 1/4 cup (60 ml) additional sugar

### Directions:

1. Preheat the oven to 170 ºC (325 ºF).
2. In a saucepan, heat the cream for 5 minutes with the vanilla bean cut in half, without boiling, just to infuse the vanilla. Scrape out the vanilla seeds and combine with the milk. If you are not using a vanilla bean, simply add the vanilla extract to the hot cream.
3. In a bowl, whisk together the egg yolks and sugar. Stir in the hot cream. Pour the mixture into four 180 ml (3/4 cup) crème brûlée dishes or ramekins.
4. Bake in a water bath. To do this, place the custard dishes in a baking dish. Pour hot water 3/4 up the sides of the dishes. Bake for 40 minutes.
5. Let cool and refrigerate to chill completely. Sprinkle with the additional sugar and caramelize quickly with a crème brûlée iron or a torch (ideally, remove from the fridge for 30 minutes before bruleeing and just before serving). You could also caramelize the crème brûlée under the broiler of a very hot oven. Serve immediately or place in the fridge for up to 3 days and it’s just as lovely served cold!

Variations:

- You can cut the sugar a bit so it is a little less sweet.
- Replacing 1/3 of heavy cream with Irish Cream (and reducing sugar in half) makes a deliciously boozy version
- Lemon: use the zest of 2 lemons and add to the cold cream and warm together. After the hot cream and egg yolks have been blended, add the juice of 1 lemon and ½ tsp of vanilla extract.
- Amaretto: add 2 tbsp of Amaretto and ¼ tsp Almond extract to cream mixture and warm together. Optional: garnish with lightly toasted slivered almonds.
