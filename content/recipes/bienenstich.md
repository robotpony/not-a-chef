This is one of our cherished family recipes. It's a light, sweet dessert, that serves well with coffee or tea.

> This is a Rille Family Recipe!  Oma Rille’s sister, Erika, used to make this cake for each family gathering and it was always a family favourite.  It was almost not a Rille Family gathering without Bienenstich.  I had asked my Great Auntie Erika for this recipe a few times and she replied that she thought she had already given it to me.  Then one day, I received a letter from Great Auntie Erika; she had written out the recipe and on the bottom of the recipe had written, “Good Luck, Happy Baking, Enjoy. Auntie Erika.  Love you.  Tell me how you made out.”  Auntie Erika passed away before I ever had a chance to make the recipe and let her know who delicious it was!  Now I try to make Bienenstich for Rille Family gatherings or events.  I’m going to write out the recipe in the format of Auntie Erika’s letter. Quick ingredient list is condensed at the bottom of the page.

> Ann Marie - I hope you can make head & tails out of all this - It’s easy after your first try.

> Good Luck, Happy Baking Enjoy! Auntie Erika.  Love you.


## Ingredients 

### Cake

- 2 cups white sugar
- 5 Eggs
- 2 ½ cups flour
- 3 tsp baking powder
- 1 ¼ cup milk
- 3 tbsp margarine (or butter)
- 1 tsp vanilla 

### Topping

- 10 tbsp brown sugar
- 4 tbsp margarine (or butter)
- 6 tbsp whipping cream 
- ¾ cup medium shredded coconut
- ¾ cup blanched almonds

### Filling 

- 1 ½ cup Whipping Cream
- ¼ cup Sugar
- 3 tbsp Instant Vanilla Pudding

## Method

### Cake

1. Scald milk, then add margarine and vanilla. Set aside to cool.
2. Beat sugar and eggs untill smooth. Combine with dry ingredients.
3. Bake at 400 degrees F (200 C) for 15 minutes or golden brown.  This needs a jelly roll pan. 
4. Then while the base is baking, make the topping and filling.

### Topping

1. Melt sugar, margarine in a medium saucepan, then bring to a slow boil.
2. Spread on hot cake
3. Put under broiler for a few minutes.  Watch carefully as it it browns fast.  Aim for a golden hue.
4. Cool on a rack, then put cake in the freezer for 1 - 2 hours.
5. Take the cake out of freezer, loosen it from the pan, and take it out of the pan.  Cut cake in half, and fill with cream filling.

### Cream Filling:

1. Beat until firm, not too firm.  Refrigerate for 10 - 15 minutes.
2. Spread on bottom of cake; place top layer on cake.
3. Place cake in a clean jelly pan, keep in the fridge.

## Notes

- I usually scald the milk before I make the batter because it takes a little while to cool.
- We use a half baking sheet; and line it with parchment paper for easier removal,
- It freezes well and is very good for company
