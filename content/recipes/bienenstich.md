This is a Rille Family Recipe!  Oma Rille’s sister, Erika, used to make this cake for each family gathering and it was always a family favourite.  It was almost not a Rille Family gathering without Bienenstich.  I had asked my Great Auntie Erika for this recipe a few times and she replied that she thought she had already given it to me.  Then one day, I received a letter from Great Auntie Erika; she had written out the recipe and on the bottom of the recipe had written, “Good Luck, Happy Baking, Enjoy. Auntie Erika.  Love you.  Tell me how you made out.”  Auntie Erika passed away before I ever had a chance to make the recipe and let her know who delicious it was!  Now I try to make Bienenstich for Rille Family gatherings or events.  I’m going to write out the recipe in the format of Auntie Erika’s letter. Quick ingredient list is condensed at the bottom of the page.

#### Beat:

- 2 cups White Sugar
- 5 Eggs

#### Add:

- 2 ½ cups Flour
- 3 tsp Baking Powder

#### Scald:

- 1 ¼ cup Milk
- After it’s scald - Add 3 tbsp Margarine (I use Butter)
- Add Vanilla (1 tsp)
- Add to the Batter above

I usually scald the milk before I make the batter because it takes a little while to cool.

Bake at 400 degrees F (200 C) for 15 minutes or Golden Brown.  This needs a Jelly Roll Pan.  (A baking sheet; and I line it with parchment paper for easier removal.)

While the Base is Baking -

#### Mix Topping:

- In a Medium Saucepan
- 10 tbsp Brown Sugar
- 4 tbsp Margarine (I use Butter)
- 6 tbsp Whipping Cream
- ¾ cup Medium Shredded Coconut
- ¾ cup Blanched Almonds

#### Melt:

- Sugar, Whipped Cream, Margarine (Butter)

#### Add:

- Coconut and Almonds - Bring to a slow boil

Spread on Hot Cake

Put under Broiler for a few minutes.  Watch very carefully - it browns fast.  Just Golden.

#### Cool:

- Cake on Rack - then put cake in the Freezer for about 1 - 2 hours.

Take the cake out of Freezer, loosen it from the pan, and take it out of the pan.  Cut cake in half, and fill with cream filling.

#### Cream Filling:

- 1 ½ cup Whipping Cream
- ¼ cup Sugar
- 3 tbsp Instant Vanilla Pudding

Beat until firm, not too firm.  Refrigerate for 10 - 15 minutes.

Spread on Bottom of Cake; place top layer on cake.

Place cake in a clean Jelly Pan - Keep in the fridge.

It will freeze well and it’s very good for company.

Ann Marie - I hope you can make head & tails out of all this - It’s easy after your first try.

Good Luck

        Happy Baking

                Enjoy

                        Auntie Erika.  Love you.

#### Ingredients:

- 2 cups + ¼ cup White Sugar (2 ¼ cups)
- 5 Eggs

- 2 ½ cups Flour
- 3 tsp Baking Powder

- 1 ¼ cup Milk
- 3 tbsp + 4 tbsp Butter (7 tbsp = 100g)
- 1 tsp Vanilla

- 10 tbsp Brown Sugar
- 6 tbsp + 1 ½ cup Whipping Cream (444ml)
- ¾ cup Medium Shredded Coconut
- ¾ cup Blanched Almonds

- 3 tbsp Instant Vanilla Pudding
