This is Bruce’s lazy cottage pie. Cottage pie is a Shepherd's pie, but with less Shepherd; a casserole layered with beef, vegetables, gravy, and covered in mashed potatoes. This is a quick recipe we make when we have leftover mashed potatoes, using our prepped softened ground beef and frozen vegetables.

#### Ingredients:

- 500g - 1kg [softened ground beef mix](https://docs.google.com/document/d/18B_6jJlS8MKhUsXeGs2gdse1qjwkUURhE54Xlw64Nuk/edit#heading=h.3fsk0afek31n) (beef, mushrooms, onions, garlic)
- 500g frozen vegetables
- 500g potatoes (mashed)
- 100ml stock or water and bouillon
- 75g cheddar (or any) cheese
- 75ml roux
- Seasoning to taste (salt, pepper, thyme, rosemary)

### Directions:

1. Make mashed potatoes however you like them. I usually add some onion and cauliflower, with butter and cheese.
2. Heat ground beef mix through. Add stock, enough volume for gravy. Add roux in parts until a gravy forms. Season to taste.
3. Preheat the oven to 190-220C (375-425F).
4. Layer a casserole dish, with beef/gravy mix, frozen veg, and finish by spooning the mashed potatoes over top until covered. Level the potatoes carefully, and texture with a fork. Top with some cheese (if desired).
5. Heat in a preheated oven until the top browns and bubbles appear along the sides. Remove from the oven and let rest for ~10 minutes.

This recipe freezes well, but you will either need to defrost or heat more gently (for longer).
