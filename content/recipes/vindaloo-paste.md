## Mechanic

The fenugreek and dried chilies are what make this paste taste like vindaloo rather than a generic curry paste. **Toast all whole spices first**: the heat activates their oils and deepens every flavour in the final dish.

## Ingredients

- 50g fresh ginger (2 inches), roughly chopped
- 3 cloves garlic, roughly chopped
- 10 dried red chilies (adjust heat to taste)
- 2 teaspoons coriander seeds
- 1 teaspoon fenugreek seeds
- 1 teaspoon cumin seeds
- 6 green cardamom pods
- 6 whole cloves
- 1 cinnamon stick
- ¼ teaspoon black peppercorns
- 3 tablespoons lemon juice

## Method

Toast whole spices (coriander, fenugreek, cumin, cardamom, cloves, cinnamon, peppercorns) in a dry pan over medium heat until fragrant, 2–3 minutes. Cool.

Grind toasted spices to a fine powder.

Blend ginger, garlic, dried chilies, and ground spices with lemon juice until a smooth paste forms, adding a splash of water if needed.

## To serve

Rub onto pork, lamb, or chicken. Marinate at least 2 hours, overnight preferred. Use the paste as the base of a braise or curry sauce.

## Notes

- Lemon juice is the traditional souring agent; some versions use tamarind or vinegar.
- Fenugreek is non-negotiable; its bitterness is a defining feature of the style.
- Keeps refrigerated for a week or frozen for a month.
