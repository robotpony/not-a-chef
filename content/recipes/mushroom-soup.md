## Mechanic

Two methods for different results. **Velouté**: a roux-thickened cream soup, rich and smooth. **Potage**: a blended potato-thickened version finished with cashew cream — dairy-free and lighter but equally silky. The mushroom base is the same for both.

## Ingredients

### Mushroom base (both methods)

- 500g mixed mushrooms (cremini, shiitake, or whatever's available), sliced
- 1 onion, diced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tbsp butter or olive oil
- 1 tbsp fresh thyme leaves
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 250ml dry white wine or dry sherry
- 750ml vegetable or chicken broth
- salt and black pepper

### For velouté

- 30g butter (2 tbsp)
- 30g flour (¼ cup)
- 120ml cream (½ cup)

### For potage (dairy-free)

- 1 medium potato, diced
- 80ml cashew cream (⅓ cup)

## Method

**Mushroom base:** Melt butter or oil in a heavy pot over medium-high heat. Add mushrooms in a single layer — don't crowd. Cook without stirring until deeply browned, about 5 minutes. Stir and brown the other side.

Add onion and cook until softened. Add garlic and thyme; cook 1 minute.

Add soy sauce and wine. Scrape up the brown bits and cook until the wine reduces by half.

Add broth and simmer for 15 minutes.

---

**Velouté:** In a separate small pan, melt butter over medium heat. Whisk in flour and cook, stirring, for 2 minutes. Whisk this roux into the simmering soup. Cook until thickened, 5 minutes. Stir in cream. Blend partially (leave some texture) or fully. Season and serve.

---

**Potage:** Add the diced potato to the broth with the mushrooms. Simmer until potato is fully soft, about 20 minutes total. Blend fully. Stir in cashew cream. Season and serve.

## To serve

Topped with a drizzle of cream or cashew cream, sautéed mushrooms, fresh thyme, and cracked black pepper. With crusty bread.

## Notes

- Dried porcini (soaked in hot water, liquid strained and added to broth) deepens the mushroom flavour significantly.
- The velouté version reheats better; potage can thin out slightly on reheating.
