I like to save wings from the whole chickens that I roast for weekly dinners by stashing them in the freezer until I have enough for a wing day. In 3-4 weeks I’ll have 5 birds worth of wings, which when split at the elbow makes for 20 winglets. This is enough for 2-3 people, assuming you serve it with some veg.

#### Ingredients:

- ~20 winglets

#### Spicy poultry rub:

- ½ cup of [poultry rub](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1HId__nmRdQcgDVy6kMU66-71jsW_a5IVgzPcJeqqHf8/edit)
- 1 tablespoon of sugar
- 1-2 teaspoons of crushed red pepper flakes (break down in a mortar and pestle)
- 1 teaspoon of smoked and dried habanero
- Optional (if you like your wings crispier)

    - 1 teaspoon baking powder
    - 1 tablespoon cornstarch

#### Hot sauce:

- ¼ cup vinegar-based hot sauce (like Frank’s)
- ¼ cup melted butter
- (to taste) hot poultry rub
- 2 tablespoons of some sort of herbal greenery (freeze dried works in a pinch)

    - Celery leaves are great, or dill, oregano, parsley, etc.

### Directions:

Prep: 20 minutes

Cook time: 20-30 minutes

Total time: 2-10 hours

1. If you have whole wings, break down each one along each joint with a sharp knife, separating the tips (to save for stock). If your wings are frozen, defrost.
2. Season the wings liberally with the spicy chicken rub and let sit in the fridge for 1-8 hours. Place on a rack in a lined baking sheet and preheat and oven to 219C (425F) with the convection on if you have it.
3. Bake wings for 20-30 minutes, turning the pan once. Flip the wings once they lift without tearing, and return to the oven for another 20-30 minutes, or until brown. They should register ~85C (~185F) on an instant read thermometer.

Note that frozen wings from younger birds may ooze a bit of red, this is harmless and does not indicate the wings are underdone (it’s from the bones). Fresh is better, and air-chilled will result in a much crispier wing.
