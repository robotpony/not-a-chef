## Mechanic

A **percentage-based spice formula**: scale every ingredient against the total weight of ground pork. This makes it trivially scalable and lets you dial individual spices. Milk powder improves binding, moisture, and subtle dairy richness. Lean toward sage; the formula is sage-forward by design.

## Ingredients (as % of pork weight)

| Ingredient | % | Example: 1 kg pork |
|---|---|---|
| Salt | 2.0% | 20g |
| Dried sage | 0.6% | 6g |
| Black pepper | 0.4% | 4g |
| Dry parsley | 0.3% | 3g |
| Dried thyme | 0.3% | 3g |
| Ground coriander | 0.3% | 3g |
| Fresh garlic, minced | 0.3% | 3g |
| Red pepper flakes | 0.2% | 2g |
| Ground fennel | 0.2% | 2g |
| MSG | 0.2% | 2g |
| Milk powder | 4.0% | 40g |
| Water (cold) | 10.0% | 100g |

## Method

Combine all dry spices and milk powder. Dissolve in the cold water.

Add to ground pork and mix until the seasoning is evenly distributed and the mixture has some tackiness, about 3 minutes by hand or stand mixer.

Refrigerate for at least 30 minutes before forming patties or filling casings.

## To serve

Formed as patties (pan-fry in a little oil over medium heat until browned through), or as bulk sausage broken into pieces.

## Notes

- The sage is intentionally high relative to fennel; adjust if you prefer an Italian-style lean.
- Marjoram can be added at 0.2–0.3% for a softer, more floral profile.
- Milk powder is the non-obvious ingredient; don't skip it.
- Softer grind (if grinding yourself) and slightly less fennel than a typical Italian sausage are the target.
