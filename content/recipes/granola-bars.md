## Mechanic

The binding ratio is **dates : oats : nuts at 1:2.5:1** by weight, held together with nut butter and maple syrup. Mashed banana adds adhesion. The mixture sets in the fridge without baking; a short bake firms the bars further if you prefer less chew.

## Ingredients

- 100g dates, pitted and mashed
- 250g rolled oats, toasted
- 100g mixed nuts, chopped and toasted
- 77g maple syrup
- 25g nut butter (almond or peanut)
- 50g banana, mashed
- spices to taste (cinnamon, cardamom)

## Method

Toast oats in a dry pan or at 175°C (350°F) for 10–12 minutes, stirring once, until lightly golden. Cool. Toast nuts separately until fragrant. Cool and chop.

Mash dates until smooth. Combine with maple syrup, nut butter, and banana. Mix until uniform.

Add oats, nuts, and spices. Mix well; the mixture should hold when pressed.

Press firmly into a lined 20×20 cm pan. Refrigerate at least 2 hours before slicing.

## To serve

Cut into bars. Keep refrigerated; they soften at room temperature.

## Notes

- Tested once: held together, assembled in under 20 minutes. Toast the nuts next time. More banana may improve binding. [draft]
- More dates makes chewier bars; more syrup makes them softer.
- Toasting the oats is essential for flavour; don't skip it.
