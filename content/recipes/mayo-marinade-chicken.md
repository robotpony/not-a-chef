## Mechanic

Mayo is the fat and emulsifier in one. It coats the meat evenly and holds the seasonings in contact throughout marinating, producing more consistent browning and a tender surface. The baking soda effect is mild; the big win is surface adhesion.

## Ingredients

- 50g mayonnaise
- 4 cloves garlic (20g minced)
- ~2g black pepper (20 cranks)
- 2 tsp dried oregano
- zest of 1 lemon (or a squeeze of lemon juice)
- 6g kosher salt
- 2g MSG

## Method

Combine all ingredients and mix well. Coat chicken and marinate for 30 minutes minimum, up to 8 hours in the fridge.

Grill, broil, or pan-fry as usual. The mayo coating browns and crisps on contact with heat.

## To serve

On chicken breasts, thighs, or skewers. Also works on pork.

## Notes

- Tested as a pure marinade (not salted separately first). Worked well; slightly salty, very tasty.
- Reduce salt to 4g if using a thinner cut that will marinate longer.
