This is a sort of copy-cat recipe of the BK classic chicken sandwich, which is a lightly breaded, pepper-forward, minced patty made from mostly white meat. I like them with fresh onion, mustard, lettuce, pickles, tomatoes, and mayonnaise. The goal is something with a few layers of crunch, some bite, and a good kick of savoury fatty goodness.

This is a work in progress and I’m tracking my [experiments in a spreadsheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1D9Ls2SRHtvSBeVAVhLJZC5CSqrhxuTvu3vb2kW5LvrU/edit#gid=0). The goal is to find a balance of savoury, salty, and spice, with the desired texture.

## Patties (9 x 90g patties)

Dissolve salt, baking soda, and MSG in water, and mix into chicken thoroughly.

Form into 2 patties and rest in the refrigerator (or freezer) on lightly oiled parchment for 20 minutes or up to a few hours.

Make sure the patties are ~1 - 2 cm wider than the buns you plan on using.

## Breading  
(consider a standard panko/crumb instead?)

Combine the flour, pepper, onion, and garlic powder.

Combine water, cornstarch and salt into a loose batter.

Dredge patties in flour and dip into the batter. Let batter drip off, then roll in breadcrumbs until maximally covered. Let sit for 10-20 minutes.

Shallow fry on medium (175-190C) until both sides are nicely browned and the internal temperature hits 70C. Carry-on heat will bring patties up to the recommended 75C.

## Variations

Classic: mayo with lettuce. Chicken in the middle.The Bruce: mayo with lettuce + tomato (top), extra pickles/onions, chicken, and a good amount of mustard.

Swiss: mayo, lettuce + tomato (top), swiss cheese, ham, chicken, mustard, and pickle.

Italian parm: mayo, lettuce + tomato (top), mozza, parmesan, chicken, and marinara.

Rancher style: mayo, lettuce + tomato (top), cheddar, onions, chicken, BBQ sauce.
