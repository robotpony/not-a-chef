These are common spice blends for UK-style Indian curries.

## Garam masala

- 2 tbsp green cardamom seeds (from shelled pods)
- 1 tsp black peppercorns
- 3 tbsp black cumin seeds
- ½ tsp whole cloves
- ⅓ whole nutmeg
- 1 medium Ceylon cinnamon stick, broken into pieces
- 1 medium stick cassia bark
- 1 tsp fennel seeds
- 1 star anise
- 4 tbsp coriander seeds
- ¾ tsp ground bay leaves

Toast the whole spices in a dry pan over medium-low heat, stirring, until fragrant, 2–3 minutes. Let them cool. Grind them fine with the nutmeg, then stir in the ground bay leaves.

## Base spice mix

- 6 tbsp turmeric
- 6 tbsp ground coriander
- 6 tbsp ground cumin
- 6 tbsp paprika
- 2 tbsp garam masala (above)
- 10 tbsp Supreme Bassar curry masala

Mix everything together. Toast very lightly in a dry pan over low heat, stirring, until just fragrant.

## Ginger/garlic paste

- 30 g ginger
- 30 g garlic
- about 2 tbsp water

Blend until smooth. This is the quick version; for a batch that keeps for weeks, see [[Ginger/garlic paste]].
