These are common bases for most UK-style Indian curries.

#### Ginger/garlic paste:

- 30g ginger
- 30g garlic
- ~2 tablespoons water

Blend in a mixer until smooth.

#### Base spice mix:

- 6 tablespoons turmeric
- 6 tablespoons coriander
- 6 tablespoons cumin
- 6 tablespoons paprika
- 2 tablespoons of garam masala (see below)
- 10 tablespoons of Supreme Bassar curry masala

Toast very lightly.

#### Garam masala:

- 1 tablespoon cardamom seeds (or shell green cardamoms)
- 1 tablespoon cardamom seeds (?)
- 1 teaspoon black peppercorns
- 3 tablespoons black cumin seeds
- ½ tsp whole cloves
- ⅓ of a whole nutmeg
- 1 medium stick cinnamon (broken into pieces)
- 1 medium stick cassia bark (ceylon cinnamon)
- 1 teaspoon fennel seeds
- 1 star anise
- 4 tablespoons coriander seeds
- ¾ teaspoon powdered bay leaves
