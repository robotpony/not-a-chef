This is a work in progress recipe to replicate Farmer Soup (from the ABC Family restaurants). This needs to use farmer sausage from Rempel Meats in Abbotsford, or a large hand-made, smoked farmer sausage.

#### Ingredients:

- 4 cups chicken broth
- ½ of a large farmer sausage, cut into bite size pieces
- 2 large Yukon gold potatoes, diced
- ¾ cup celery, diced
- 1 ½ cups of carrots, diced
- 1 cup onions, diced
- Salt + pepper (to taste)
- 1 tbsp herbs de provence
- 1-2 tbsp soy sauce

#### Thicken with:

- 3 tbsp corn starch
- 1 cup cream
- 1 cup whole  milk

### Directions:

1. Fry farmer sausage and brown slightly. Add onions and saute until translucent. Add remaining veg, broth, and seasonings and stir. Lower heat and let simmer until potatoes soften.
2. Add corn starch to milk and cream and combine. Add to simmered soup and stir until thickened slightly.
3. Salt and pepper to taste and serve.
