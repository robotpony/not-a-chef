## Ingredients

- 250g whole-milk ricotta (avoid brands with stabilizers)
- 4 cloves garlic, finely minced
- 1 shallot, finely minced
- 2 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
- 2 tbsp heavy cream
- 1–2 egg yolks
- pinch dried oregano
- pinch dried thyme
- salt, black pepper, MSG

## Method

Combine ricotta, garlic, shallot, olive oil, and cream in a bowl. Mix until smooth. Add egg yolks and season with salt, pepper, MSG, oregano, and thyme. Stir to combine.

Spread on flatbread or pizza dough before adding toppings. The egg yolk sets as the flatbread bakes, thickening and enriching the sauce.

## To serve

Under mushrooms, roasted garlic, or prosciutto on flatbread. Also good as a dip.

## Notes

- Adapted loosely from an ATK white pizza sauce.
- 1 egg yolk gives a lighter result; 2 is richer and slightly sturdier.
- Avoid stabilized ricotta (such as brands with guar gum); it doesn't blend as smoothly and can separate.
