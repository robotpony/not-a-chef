Makes ~5 cups of spice, and works well on pork and chicken.  It's sweet, savoury, and has lemon and smoky notes.

See the [precise poultry mix sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1D9Ls2SRHtvSBeVAVhLJZC5CSqrhxuTvu3vb2kW5LvrU/edit?gid=1772331421#gid=1772331421) here for a weight based recipe. Approximate ingredients:

- 1 cup kosher salt (270g, 450g = 1 ⅔ cup)
- 2 tsp Accent (MSG) (9g, 18g = 2x)
- 2 cups brown sugar (180g, 1 cup)
- ½ cup white sugar (100g)

- 4 tbsp poultry seasoning (any of ground/chopped rosemary, sage, thyme, marjoram)
- 1 tbsp black pepper
- 1 tbsp white pepper
- 1 tbsp ground coriander seed

### Directions:

1. Throw ingredients in a large sealable container, after grinding anything that isn't fairly fine. Make sure the brown sugar isn't clumped up by smashing with a fork.
2. Mix. Mix some more. Taste. It should be lovely.
