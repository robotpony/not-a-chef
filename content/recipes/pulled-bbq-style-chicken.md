This is a simple pulled or shredded BBQ style chicken that can be made without a smoker or BBQ. This is a braised dish that can be made on the stovetop, in the oven, in a crock pot, or in a pressure cooker. I prefer making it in the oven, in covered hotel pans.

See also: [Shredded taco chicken](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1EBgVeqvXsort1FnW5urv4GntB0neI4kBYvrDw-7aGVo/edit?tab=t.0#heading=h.3fsk0afek31n)

## Ingredients:

- 1kg chicken thighs, quartered (boneless, skinless)
- 1kg chicken breasts, cubed (boneless, skinless)
- 1 teaspoons liquid smoke
- 1.5 tablespoons poultry [rub](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1HId__nmRdQcgDVy6kMU66-71jsW_a5IVgzPcJeqqHf8/edit#heading=h.jnuahajl5cre)
- 1.5 teaspoon bouillon powder

Rub liquid smoke, spices, bouillon, salt, over chicken and set aside in the fridge for 30 minutes or for up to 4 hours.

## Braising liquid:

- 250ml water or stock
- 125ml cider vinegar  
    (sub with a light beer, white wine, etc.)
- ½ cup brown sugar or maple syrup
- 4 cloves garlic (smashed)
- ½ onion roughly chopped

## Method:

### Stovetop:

1. In a dutch oven or large pot, combine braising liquid and stir to combine on low heat.
2. Optionally, use a separate pan to sear the chicken to add texture and flavour.
3. Add chicken to braising liquid and simmer (medium/low) , cooking to an internal temperature of 70C/160F.
4. Remove from heat and let rest for 10 minutes. Remove chicken from pot (saving the liquid). Shred with a fork, or by using tongs to crush the chicken pieces.
5. Add back enough braising liquid to keep the chicken moist.
6. Optionally: reduce the braising liquid after straining, and thicken (and season) as a sauce.

### Alternative methods:

- Pressure cooker: ~15 minutes on high
- Oven: 30-40 minutes in hotel pan covered with foil, at 160C/325F
