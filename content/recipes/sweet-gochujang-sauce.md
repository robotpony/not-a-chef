This is a vibrant, and tasty sauce for wings, chicken sandwiches, noodles, and tofu. It’s savoury, sweet, and mildly spicy (according to our family).

#### Ingredients:

- ¼-½ cup gochujang
- 2 tablespoons of ketchup
- 3 tablespoons of rice wine, rice wine vinegar, or vinegar
- 2 tablespoons of soy sauce (dark or regular)
- 2 tablespoons honey, brown sugar, or maple syrup
- 1-2 cloves of garlic finely minced (or granulated garlic)
- 1-2 teaspoons ground ginger (or equivalent minced)
- ½ teaspoon 5 spice

### Directions:

1. Combine ingredients in a saucepan, whisk, and heat, reducing until thickened.
2. Remove from heat, let cool enough to handle, and use on your favourite dish (or store in the fridge for up to a few weeks).

    1. Drizzle over fried tofu, or
    2. Drizzle on roast veggies, or
    3. Toss with lightly seasoned oven-baked chicken wings, etc.
