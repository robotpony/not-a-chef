A cooked, glossy gochujang glaze for wings, chicken sandwiches, noodles, and tofu. It's savoury, sweet, and mildly spicy (according to our family). For an uncooked version, see [[Gochujang wing sauce]].

## Ingredients

- ¼–½ cup gochujang
- 2 tbsp ketchup
- 3 tbsp rice wine, rice vinegar, or vinegar
- 2 tbsp soy sauce (dark or regular)
- 2 tbsp honey, brown sugar, or maple syrup
- 1–2 cloves garlic, finely minced (or granulated garlic)
- 1–2 tsp ground ginger, or 1 tbsp fresh, minced
- ½ tsp five-spice powder

## Method

Whisk everything together in a saucepan. Heat and reduce until thickened.

Take it off the heat and let it cool enough to handle.

## To serve

Drizzled over fried tofu or roast veggies, or tossed with lightly seasoned oven-baked chicken wings.

## Notes

- Keeps in the fridge for up to a few weeks.
