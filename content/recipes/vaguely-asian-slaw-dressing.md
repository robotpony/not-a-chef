This is a great base dressing for an Asian style slaw or chunky salad. You can add chilis for kick, or ginger/garlic depth.

## Ingredients:

- 90g extra-virgin olive (or a nice/neutral) oil
- 50g tablespoons seasoned rice vinegar
- 30g Dijon mustard
- 15g sesame oil
- 15g soy sauce
- 10g maple syrup
- 2g MSG
- 2g finely minced ginger (jarred is fine)

Combine ingredients with an immersion blender or by hand and refrigerate. Season to taste (salt, pepper, etc.).
