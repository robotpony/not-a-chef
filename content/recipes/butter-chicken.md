## Mechanic

This is a BIR-style (British Indian Restaurant) butter chicken — built on a pre-made base curry gravy rather than starting from scratch. The gravy does the heavy lifting; this recipe adds the butter, cream, and finishing flavours that make it distinctly butter chicken.

## Ingredients

- 500 ml [Base Curry Gravy](Base Curry Gravy) <!-- TODO: base curry gravy recipe not yet in vault — was linked to Google Doc -->
- 4 tbsp almond powder
- 4 tbsp yogurt or cream
- 2 tbsp oil (onion oil preferred)
- 40g butter, plus more to finish
- 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste
- 2 tsp masala
- 1 tbsp tomato paste (optional)
- 2 tsp methi (dried fenugreek leaves)
- 500g chicken thigh or breast, diced and precooked

To finish:
- salt to taste
- additional butter
- toasted nuts
- fresh coriander leaves

## Method

Heat oil and butter in a large pan over medium. Add ginger-garlic paste and stir until fragrant, about 1 minute. Add masala and stir until fragrant, about 1 minute. Add tomato paste if using and stir in.

Add almond powder and stir until combined and fragrant, 1–2 minutes. <!-- TODO: original mentions adding sugar here but sugar is not in the ingredient list — verify amount -->

Add the diced precooked chicken and stir to coat in the spice mixture.

Pour in the base curry gravy and stir to combine. Bring to a bare simmer.

Turn off the heat. Add yogurt or cream and stir until smooth. Note: temper the yogurt by adding a few spoonfuls of hot liquid to it first, or it may curdle.

Simmer on low until heated through. Finish with butter, salt, and methi. Taste and adjust.

## To serve

With basmati rice or [Naan](Naan). Top with toasted nuts and fresh coriander.
