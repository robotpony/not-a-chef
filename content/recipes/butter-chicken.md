This is a fairly classic takeout style butter chicken; it’s creamy, sweet, and savoury.

- 500ml [base curry gravy](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1w1DLloyZW3RzU817Vk3gYZ8EMFdazqHMiusbOB1-HC8/edit#heading=h.3fsk0afek31n)
- 4 tablespoons almond powder
- 4 tablespoons yogurt or cream
- 2 tablespoons oil (onion oil is better)
- 40g butter
- 1 teaspoon ginger/garlic paste
- 2 teaspoon masala
- 1 tablespoon tomato paste (optional)
- 2 teaspoons methi (fenugreek leaves)
- 500g diced precooked, lightly seasoned chicken (thigh or breasts)
- (optional) Steamed veg, frozen peas, etc.
- To finish:

    - Salt to taste,
    - Additional butter,
    - Toasted nuts, and
    - Coriander leaves

#### Method:

1. Add oil and 40g butter to a hot family size pan (on medium or medium/low). Stir in ginger/garlic pastes until fragrant (less than a minute). Add masala, and stir until fragrant (less than a minute). Optionally add tomato paste and stir in (ditto).
2. Add almond powder and stir until combined and fragrant (1-2 minutes). Add sugar, and stir.
3. Add diced, cooked chicken and stir into oil/spices/almond powder. Will smell lovely. Cook until the chicken is warmed.
4. Add ~500ml of base curry gravy, and stir until combined and warmed to a bare simmer.
5. Turn off heat, and add yogurt or cream and stir until homogenous.  
    Note: cream and yogurt can curdle quickly, so either temper, or reduce heat.
6. Simmer until heated through.
7. Optionally add finishing butter, salt, and indian chilli powder to taste. Plate with rice or naan, and top with toasted nuts/coriander leaves.
