#### Fruit filling:

- 2 small cans of peaches and juice

    - Frozen fruit + fruit juice works as well

- ½ cup white sugar (or to taste)
- 1 tablespoon cornstarch
- 1 tablespoon peach schnapps (optional)
- 1 teaspoon cinnamon
- ½ teaspoon nutmeg
- 1 teaspoon lemon juice
- 1 teaspoon salt

#### Crumble:

- 1 cup cold butter, diced or grated
- ½ cup flour
- 1 ½ cup rolled oats
- ⅓ cup brown sugar
- ½ cup nuts (pecans, crushed)
- 4 tablespoons cinnamon
- 2 teaspoons salt

### Method:

1. Make a thickened sauce with the peach juice, cinnamon, lemon, schnapps, lemon juice, salt, and sugar. Combine in a cold sauce pan and whisk until cornstarch is combined. Heat on low until thickened. Add peaches and set aside to cool.
2. Combine crumble ingredients by hand until dry ingredients are incorporated in the butter and are crumbly. Optionally you can pulse in a food processor or mix with the paddle attachment on a stand mixer.
3. Add fruit and sauce to a medium baking dish. Cover with crumble.
4. Bake at 190C for 45 minutes, or until crisp.
