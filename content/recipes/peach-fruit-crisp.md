This is a classic summer treat, with peaches from the Okanagan.

## Fruit filling

- 2 cans peaches in juice (398 ml / 14 oz each), drained with the juice reserved
- 100g white sugar (½ cup), or to taste
- 1 tablespoon cornstarch
- 1 tablespoon peach schnapps (optional)
- 1 teaspoon cinnamon
- ½ teaspoon nutmeg
- 1 teaspoon lemon juice
- 1 teaspoon salt

Whisk the reserved juice, sugar, cornstarch, schnapps, cinnamon, nutmeg, lemon juice, and salt together in a cold saucepan until no lumps of cornstarch remain. Heat on low, stirring, until the sauce turns glossy and thick enough to coat a spoon. Stir in the peaches and set aside to cool.

## Crumble

- 225g cold butter (1 cup), diced or grated
- 65g all-purpose flour (½ cup)
- 135g rolled oats (1½ cups)
- 70g brown sugar (⅓ cup)
- 55g pecans (½ cup), crushed
- 4 teaspoons cinnamon
- 2 teaspoons salt

Rub the butter into the dry ingredients by hand until everything is coated and the mixture is crumbly. A few pulses in a food processor or a short mix with the paddle attachment on a stand mixer also works.

## To bake

Spread the fruit and sauce in a medium baking dish and cover with the crumble. Bake at 190°C (375°F) until the crumble is deep golden and crisp and the filling bubbles at the edges, about 45 minutes.

## Notes

- Frozen fruit with fruit juice works in place of canned peaches.
- The original recipe listed 4 tablespoons of cinnamon in the crumble, which is almost certainly a typo; 4 teaspoons is used here.
- Salt is high across the two components (3 teaspoons total). Use unsalted butter, and cut the crumble salt to 1 teaspoon if the fruit isn't very sweet.
- For a leaner, sandier topping, see [[Crisp topping]].
