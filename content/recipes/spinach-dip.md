Auntie Betty-Lou used to make this Spinach Dip and it is now Ann’s favourite version to make and is always a hit no matter where we take and serve it!  We make this for most holidays or family gatherings.

#### Main ingredients:

- 1 package of Knorr Vegetable Soup Mix
- 1 ¼ cup of Sour Cream
- 1 ¼ cup of Hellman's Mayonnaise
- 1 can Water Chestnuts (finely chopped)
- Black pepper
- 1 package of frozen Spinach, thawed
- Sourdough, crackers, or other dip vehicle

### Directions:

1. Defrost the frozen spinach overnight in the fridge.
2. Squeeze water out of spinach (if not yet completely defrosted, run under cold water)
3.  Cut up spinach and combine with soup mix, sour cream, and mayo.  Add black pepper, to taste.  Stir well!
4. Rest in the fridge; ideally for 24 hours before serving.
