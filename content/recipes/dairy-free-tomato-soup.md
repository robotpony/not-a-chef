White beans are an old world secret for making thick, hearty soups. When blended, they add a creamy texture, additional protein, without affecting the flavour negatively.

#### Ingredients:

- 1 large can (820ml/28 ounces) whole tomatoes and liquid
- 2 cups vegetable broth
- ½ cup cooked white beans, rinsed and drained  
    Baby Lima, Cannellini, Great Northern, etc.
- 1 medium yellow onion, diced
- 2 tablespoons tomato paste
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 2 tablespoons unsalted butter, cut into a few pieces (optional)
- 1 tablespoon miso (or light soy sauce)
- 1 teaspoon coconut sugar or brown sugar, to taste
- 1 teaspoon salt
- Freshly ground black pepper, to taste
- Basil (optional)

### Method:

This is a fairly standard blended soup.

1. In a large pot or dutch oven, saute onions and salt in olive oil until softened. Add tomato paste, stirring constantly, until it starts to stick.
2. Add broth and deglaze, then add the tomatoes, sugar, pepper, and beans. Bring to a bare simmer, stirring regularly for 30-60 minutes.
3. Remove from heat and let cool. Add to a stand blender and blend until smooth. Alternatively, use an immersion blender.
4. Taste and season with miso/soy, sugar, and salt to taste.
