## Mechanic

Start in a pan that has already cooked meat or meatballs: the rendered fat and browned bits in the pan are the flavour base. The tomato paste goes in before the wine to caramelize briefly; the tin of crushed tomatoes finishes it.

## Ingredients

- pan drippings from cooked meatballs or browned meat (1–2 tablespoons worth)
- 2 tablespoons tomato paste
- splash of red wine
- 400g tin whole or crushed tomatoes, mashed
- pinch dried oregano
- salt and black pepper

## Method

After removing cooked meat from the pan, set it back over medium heat. Add tomato paste and stir into the drippings for 1–2 minutes until the paste darkens slightly.

Add wine and scrape up any browned bits. Add tomatoes and oregano. Simmer 15 minutes, breaking up the tomatoes with a spoon.

Taste and adjust salt and seasoning.

## To serve

Tossed with pasta, spooned over meatballs, or as a pizza sauce base.

## Notes

- This method depends on having cooked meat or meatballs in the pan first; the drippings are the point.
- A clove of garlic added with the tomato paste is a natural extension.
