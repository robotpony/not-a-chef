## Mechanic

The **vital wheat gluten** supplements bread flour to push gluten content higher, producing a chewier crumb and stronger structure than all-purpose flour alone. Low yeast (0.1%) and a long cold ferment develop flavour. The autolyse (30-minute rest before adding salt) speeds gluten development.

## Ingredients

- 900g white bread flour
- 100g vital wheat gluten
- 700g water (70% hydration)
- 25g kosher salt
- 25g sugar
- 1g dried instant yeast

## Method

Combine flour and vital wheat gluten. Add water and mix until just combined. Cover and rest 30 minutes (autolyse).

Add salt and sugar. Mix on medium speed for 7 minutes, or knead by hand until a smooth, slightly tacky ball forms.

Divide into 175g portions. Shape into tight balls. Place in the fridge for 24–48 hours (minimum 12 hours).

Remove from fridge 30 minutes before shaping. Stretch on a lightly floured surface to 15–20 cm.

Cook at 233°C (450°F) with convection fan on a hot carbon steel pan or baking stone. 12–16 minutes depending on thickness and toppings.

## Notes

- Testing (birthday pizza 2023): best results with 1.5% yeast + long ferment, 200g dough weight per pizza, 14 min at 233°C + fan, carbon steel + parchment.
- Reducing gluten slightly (toward 12.5% total protein) gives a less chewy crumb.
- Autolyse can run 5 minutes shorter without significant difference.
