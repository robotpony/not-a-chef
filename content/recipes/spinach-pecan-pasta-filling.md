This is a great filling for vegetarian pasta like ravioli, tortellini, or sauced pastas like lasagne or ragu. Makes enough filling for around 50 small stuffed pastas (10g each).

#### Ingredients:

- 250ml ricotta cheese
- 60g thawed chopped spinach
- 40g crushed pecans
- 15g (2 cloves) smashed/minced garlic
- 25g grated parmesan cheese (~ ½ cup) plus more for serving
- 20g fresh or dried herbs (thyme, basil, oregano, etc.)
- 5g freshly ground black pepper
- 3g salt
- Butter or oil for frying

## Method:

1. Fry nuts, shallots and garlic until translucent. Add peppers, spinach, and seasonings, cooking and stirring until they start to dry out. Deglaze (if needed), scrape up any fond, and let cool.
2. Combine ricotta, parmesan, and cooled mixture thoroughly.
3. Cool before using as a pasta filling.

If you roll the cooled filling out and freeze, you can slice it up  in 5-10g chunks depending on the size of pasta you plan to fill..
