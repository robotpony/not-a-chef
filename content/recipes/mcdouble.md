## Mechanic

The McDouble is built on two thin patties, a single slice of cheese between them, and a sharp dressing ratio. **Double-grinding** the beef is what gives the patty its soft, almost pillowy texture. 

Smash-and-freeze before cooking locks the shape. Press between parchment sheets using to flat surfaces, until 5-10% wider than your target bun. Par freeze before cooking.

## Ingredients

### Patties

- 150g ground beef, double-ground (two 75g patties)
- salt, black pepper (applied after cooking)

### Dressing (per burger)

- 2 teaspoons ketchup
- ½ teaspoon mustard (5:1 ketchup to mustard)
- 2 sour pickles, thinly sliced
- 1 teaspoon dried minced onion (or fresh onion soaked in cold water and drained)

### Build

- 1 slice American cheese
- 1 small bun (~9 cm), slightly pressed flat

## Method

Form two balls of double-ground beef. Flatten into patties slightly larger than the bun (they will shrink). Freeze on a flat tray until firm, 20–30 minutes.

Cook from frozen on a hot griddle or cast iron pan. Press flat as they hit the pan if you want a smash texture. Cook until well-browned on the bottom, 2–3 minutes. Flip and add cheese to one patty. Cook another minute.

Season with salt and pepper immediately after cooking.

Stack patties with cheese between them. Apply dressing, pickles, and onion. Close bun.

## To serve

Immediately. These hold badly.

## Notes

- The 5:1 ketchup:mustard ratio gives that distinctive fast-food sweet-savory balance.
- Double-ground beef: pulse the ground beef through the grinder twice, or blitz briefly in a food processor.
- Bun pressed to ~11 cm (4⅓ inch) matches the patty diameter after smashing.
- The restaurant version uses45g patties, but they're a pain to work with at that size at home
