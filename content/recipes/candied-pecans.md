A sweet little treat!  It’s a great snack to put out for a get-together or as a pre-treat.

#### Ingredients:

- ½ cup Sugar
- ½ tsp Cinnamon
- ½ tsp Salt
- 1 Egg White
- 2 tsp cold Water
- 5 cups Pecans, whole

### Directions:

1. Preheat the oven to 225 degrees F (110 C).
2. Mix together the Sugar, Cinnamon, and Salt; ensure it is mixed well.
3. Separately in a large bowl, whip together the Egg White with the cold Water until frothy.
4. Add the Pecans to the egg white mixture and coat.
5. Sprinkle the sugar/cinnamon mixture over the coated pecans.
6. Evenly spread the coated Pecans onto a large baking sheet.
7. Bake for 1 hour; turning the Pecans every 15 minutes.
