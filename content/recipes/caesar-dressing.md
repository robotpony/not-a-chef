## Mechanic

This is a blended emulsion: the egg white and Dijon suspend the olive oil. Drizzle the oil slowly with the blender running so it incorporates rather than pools. The 2 tablespoons of sugar is not a mistake — it's the family version, rounding out the anchovy and lemon without making the dressing noticeably sweet.

## Ingredients

- 1 clove garlic, crushed
- 1 egg white
- 1 tsp anchovy paste (or Worcestershire sauce)
- 1 tsp fresh lemon juice
- 2 tsp dried basil
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 2 tbsp sugar
- ¼ tsp black pepper
- 2 tbsp Dijon mustard
- 2–3 tbsp good olive oil
- 1 tsp Accent (optional)

## Method

Add everything except the olive oil to a blender. Blend until smooth. With the blender running, drizzle in the olive oil until the dressing is thick enough to coat a spoon.

## To serve

Romaine, croutons, shaved Parmesan.

## Notes

- From Auntie Linda Stout.
- Keeps refrigerated 3–4 days.
