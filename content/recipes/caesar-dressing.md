Our family version of the famous dressing, from Auntie Linda Stout.

#### Main ingredients:

- 1 clove garlic (crushed or minced)
- 1 egg white
- 1 tsp anchovy paste (or worcestershire sauce)
- 1 tsp lemon juice (fresh)
- 2 tsp basil
- 1 tsp oregano
- 2 tbsp sugar
- ¼ tsp black pepper
- 2 tbsp Dijon mustard (to help emulsify)
- 2-3 tbsp nice olive oil
- 1 tsp Accent (optional)

### Directions:

1. Blend ingredients in a blender, drizzling the olive oil until consistency is smooth and thick enough to cover a spoon.
