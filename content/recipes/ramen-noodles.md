This is a simple chewy noodle recipe perfect for ramen, based on the [Serious Eats base recipe](https://www.seriouseats.com/homemade-ramen-noodles) with some tweaks from [Ramen Lord’s book](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1qLPoLxek3WLQJDtU6i3300_0nNioqeYXi7vESrtNvjQ/edit). These noodles will start out very shaggy, but will come together after resting.

Use the scaling sheet if you need more or less noodle.

## Basic noodle dough

Combine flour and gluten in a large bowl.

Dissolve salt and sodium carbonate in water.

Make a well in the middle of the flour and add the water mixture. Roughly stir together with a fork until things start to stick. Blend together with a pie dough blender/cutter, then with your hands.

Spread out the shaggy dough on some plastic wrap and press it into a wide and flat “board”, and wrap tightly.

## Working the dough

Working with the shaggy dough feels difficult at first, but it can be done with a few resting steps:

1. Work the dough into the initial shaggy board, wrap it, and press it down. Rest in the fridge for 24 hours (or on the counter for 20+ minutes).
2. Cut the dough into 4 portions, and use a rolling pin to flatten it enough to fit in your pasta maker. Carefully fold and repeat 2-3 times, and focus on laminating in one direction to encourage long gluten strand development (more [here](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1qLPoLxek3WLQJDtU6i3300_0nNioqeYXi7vESrtNvjQ/edit#)). Let rest again, wrapped tightly.
3. Work the dough further, down to the second narrowest setting on your pasta maker. Switch to a cutting blade, and run it through to noodlize it.
4. Toss the noodles in flower (or starch), and store in sealed deli containers or similar. Freezes well, lasts at least a few days in the fridge.

These noodles cook quickly, even from frozen (so no more than a few minutes).
