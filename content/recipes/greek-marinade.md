I developed this when trying to replicate a local Greek restaurant's roast chicken and potatoes. It's good enough for a weeknight meal.

## Mechanic

Built on a roughly **1:1 olive oil to lemon juice base**, with red wine vinegar added for a sharper secondary acid. It needs more lemon than 1:1 suggests; start there and taste up.

## Ingredients

- 60 ml extra-virgin olive oil
- 60 ml fresh lemon juice
- 15 ml red wine vinegar
- 1–2 cloves garlic, minced
- ½ teaspoon dried oregano
- ¼ teaspoon black pepper
- ¼ teaspoon MSG
- salt to taste

## Method

Whisk all ingredients together. Taste: it should be clearly lemony, well-seasoned, and fragrant. Adjust lemon if you want more brightness.

Coat protein and marinate: 30 minutes for thin cuts, 2–4 hours for chops, up to overnight for bone-in.

## To serve

On lamb or pork chops, chicken thighs, or fish. Doubles as a dressing for a Greek salad.

## Notes

- An onion-forward variation replaces some lemon with grated or mashed onion (1:1:0.5 oil, onion, lemon).
- Works well with marjoram added alongside the oregano.
- For a herb-heavy version that also works on rice and salads, see [[Greek lemon and herb marinade]].
