## Mechanic

Built on a roughly **1:1 olive oil to lemon juice base**, with red wine vinegar added for a sharper secondary acid. The note "needs more lemon" suggests leaning the citrus heavier than you'd expect.

## Ingredients

- 60ml extra virgin olive oil
- 60ml fresh lemon juice
- 15ml red wine vinegar
- 1–2 cloves garlic, minced
- ½ tsp dried oregano
- ¼ tsp black pepper
- ¼ tsp MSG
- salt to taste

## Method

Whisk all ingredients together. Taste: it should be clearly lemony, well-seasoned, and fragrant. Adjust lemon if you want more brightness.

Coat protein and marinate: 30 minutes for thin cuts, 2–4 hours for chops, up to overnight for bone-in.

## To serve

On lamb or pork chops, chicken thighs, or fish. Doubles as a dressing for a Greek salad.

## Notes

- Needs more lemon than a 1:1 ratio suggests. Start at 1:1 and taste up.
- An onion-forward variation replaces some lemon with grated or mashed onion (1:1:0.5 oil, onion, lemon).
- Works well with marjoram added alongside the oregano.
