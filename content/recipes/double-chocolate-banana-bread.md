We found this one when a craving for banana bread hit. Of all the recipes we looked at, the Smitten Kitchen version stood out. It has been reliable, is pretty easy, and always fills the craving.

## Ingredients

- 3 medium-to-large very ripe bananas
- ½ cup butter, melted
- ¾ cup brown sugar
- 1 large egg
- 1 tsp pure vanilla extract
- 1 tsp baking soda
- ¼ tsp salt
- ½ tsp cinnamon (optional)
- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- ½ cup cocoa powder
- 1 cup semisweet or bittersweet chocolate chunks or chips

## Method

Heat the oven to 175°C (350°F). Butter a 23×13 cm (9×5 in) loaf pan.

Mash the bananas in the bottom of a large bowl. Whisk in the melted butter, then the brown sugar, egg, and vanilla.

Put the baking soda, salt, cinnamon (if using), flour, and cocoa in a sifter or fine-mesh strainer, and sift them over the wet ingredients. Cocoa is almost always lumpy, so don't skip this; push the last of it through with the back of a spoon.

Stir with a spoon until just combined. Stir in the chocolate.

Pour the batter into the pan. Bake until a toothpick in the centre of the loaf comes out mostly batter-free, 55–65 minutes. A smear of melted chocolate is expected.

Cool in the pan for 15 minutes. Run a knife around the edge and turn the loaf out onto a cooling rack. Serve warm or at room temperature.

## Notes

- Keeps up to 4 days at room temperature, wrapped in foil, and a little longer in the fridge.
