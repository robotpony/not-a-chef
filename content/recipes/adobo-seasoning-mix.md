## Ingredients

- 2 tbsp kosher salt
- 1 tbsp sweet paprika
- 2 tsp ground black pepper
- 1½ tsp onion powder
- 1½ tsp dried oregano
- 1½ tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp chili powder

## Method

Combine all spices and store in a sealed jar. Label with the date.

## To serve

Use as a dry rub on pork, chicken, or beef. Excellent on meatballs. Also good as a seasoning base for rice, beans, or stew.

## Notes

- Mix used for meatballs; described as very good. Panade was slightly wet, which worked well.
- A small amount of smoked paprika in place of some sweet paprika adds depth for grilled applications.
