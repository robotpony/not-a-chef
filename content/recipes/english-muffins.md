Simple english muffins that Bruce very occasionally makes to feed his mcmuffin cravings.

Note: I’m still tuning this recipe.

#### Bloom:

- 1 ¾ cups lukewarm milk
- 2 ¼ tsp (1 pkg) yeast

#### Ingredients:

- 3 tbsp butter, softened
- 1 ¼ tsp salt
- 2 tbsp sugar
- 1 large egg
- 4 ½ cups flour

### Directions:

1. Add milk + yeast (and some of the flour) to a bowl, and let it stand for 10 minutes (up to 24 hours). Longer bloom times develop funk (AKA flavor).
2. Add butter, salt, sugar, egg, flour (most of it) and stir with a wooden spoon or spatula until smooth, or use a stand mixer.
3. Scrape into an oiled bowl and let rise until roughly doubled. Rising in the fridge overnight
4. Make 75g/2.5oz balls, press flat, and let rest for 10-20
5. Cook in griddle medium-low, ~6 minutes per side or until golden
6. Cool on rack and split with a fork
