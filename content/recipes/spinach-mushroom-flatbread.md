This is a quick flatbread, similar to the Trader Joe’s mushroom and truffle flatbread (but without the truffle oil).

## Ingredients:

- 2x (20cm / 8in) flatbreads, naans, etc.
- ¼ cup grated parmesan
- ½ cup grated swiss or emmental
- 1 tablespoon mayonnaise

## Topping:

- 1 cup sliced mushrooms (criminis work)
- 1 cup frozen spinach
- 3 cloves garlic
- 1 tablespoon unsalted butter (or olive oil)
- 1 teaspoon lemon juice
- ½ teaspoon thyme
- ½ teaspoon coriander
- ½ teaspoon MSG (for punch)
- Salt and pepper to taste

## Method:

1. Sauté the toppings in a medium pan:

- Add butter, mushrooms, and salt, and cook until wet (then until browning)
- Add spinach and cook until the water is gone
- Add garlic and cook until softened (but not browned)
- Add thyme, coriander, MSG, and salt/pepper (to taste)
- Set aside to cool

2. Preheat your oven with your flatbreads until they’re warmed through (and defrosted if frozen). Goal is to make sure the flatbread does not start out soggy. Remove before the bread is toasted.
3. Pull the bread out of the oven and add toppings:

- Spread a thin layer of mayo on the flatbread
- Add mushroom mixture evenly (make sure to break up any lumps of spinach)
- Top with a spartan amount of cheese
- Return to oven and bake until the bread is crispy and cheese melted
