Sometimes you just want a small portion of crisp. For science, of course.

## Ingredients

### Filling

- 500g (3 cups) fresh or frozen berries or stone fruit
- 50g granulated sugar (¼ cup)
- 2 teaspoons cornstarch (use 1½ tablespoons for a thicker filling)
- 10ml lemon juice (2 teaspoons)
- pinch of salt
- ½ teaspoon vanilla extract (for stone fruit and berry combinations)

### Topping

- 45g old-fashioned rolled oats (½ cup)
- 60g salted butter, melted (¼ cup)
- 50g brown sugar (¼ cup)
- 30g all-purpose flour (¼ cup)
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- ½ teaspoon cinnamon

## Method

Preheat oven to 180°C (350°F). Grease 4 ramekins.

Toss filling ingredients together until the fruit is evenly coated. The cornstarch will thicken as it bakes; for a thicker filling you can cook the fruit briefly on the stovetop first.

Stir topping ingredients together until the mixture is batter-like.

Divide filling into the ramekins. Spoon topping evenly over each.

Bake until the topping is golden and the filling is bubbling at the edges, 20–25 minutes. Frozen fruit takes the longer end or a few minutes more.

## To serve

Warm, with ice cream or whipped cream.

## Notes

- Tested with strawberry-rhubarb and vanilla. Good result.
- Can be made in one cake pan instead; increase baking time to 30–35 minutes.
