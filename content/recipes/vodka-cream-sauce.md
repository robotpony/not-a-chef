This was one of the first fancy recipes we made when the kids were young. It was the beginning of our shared journey into cooking new foods. I haven’t made this in ages now, so I have no idea if it’s any good. But … memories ...   

#### Ingredients:

- 2 cloves of garlic, minced
- 2 shallots, minced
- 1 cup of vodka (and 1 cup for the recipe)
- 1 cup chicken or mushroom stock (fresh if you’ve got it)
- 1 large can (1000ml/32oz) crushed tomatoes (the good ones if you can find them)
- Pasta, something with texture (like penne rigate)
- ½ cup heavy cream (AKA, whipping cream)
- 1 cup of fresh basil (20 leaves), julienned or torn
- Salt and pepper (to taste)
- Olive oil
- Butter

#### Optional ingredients:

- ½ cup Parmesan similar
- French bread (which you can make into garlic bread if you’re smart)

#### Optional proteins:

- 2 handfuls of  medium size, cleaned, peeled, and deveined shrimp, or
- 1-2 cups of uncooked sausage (something nice), or
- 1-2 cups of cleaned mushrooms

## Directions:

1. Cook optional proteins in oil, with salt and pepper (if needed). Remove from pan and reserve.
2. Heat a large skillet over medium heat. Add oil, butter, garlic, and shallots. Sauté until translucent (but not overly browned), approximately 3~5 minutes.
3. Add vodka to pan (step back if cooking over gas), and stir to pick up any fond. Reduce (by half).
4. Add chicken stock and crushed tomatoes. Season with salt and pepper to taste. Drop heat to medium-low and simmer
5. Cook pasta in salted water while the sauce simmers. Prepare salad, garlic toast, or any other sides.
6. When al dente, drain pasta and reserve 1 cup of pasta water.
7. Combine pasta with simmering sauce, stirring until combined. Remove from heat and add optional protein, cheese, ¾ of the basil, and pasta water until smooth.
8. Serve, garnishing with remaining basil.

## Variations

Some recipes have variations, like turning pizza dough into foccia. You can add such instructions at the end, or just remove this section.
