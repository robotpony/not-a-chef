## Ingredients

- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, diced
- 3 cloves garlic, sliced
- 780ml tinned whole tomatoes (2 × 400ml tins)
- 250ml chicken or vegetable broth (1 cup)
- 60ml cream (¼ cup)
- salt and black pepper

## Method

Warm the olive oil in a heavy pot over medium heat. Add onion and cook until softened and translucent, about 8 minutes.

Add garlic and cook until fragrant, about 1 minute.

Add tomatoes (crush them by hand as they go in) and broth. Bring to a simmer and cook, uncovered, for 15 minutes.

Blend until smooth with an immersion blender or in batches in a stand blender.

Return to the pot over low heat. Stir in the cream and season with salt and pepper. Taste and adjust.

## To serve

With crusty bread or a grilled cheese. A drizzle of olive oil and cracked pepper on top.

## Notes

- Tinned fire-roasted tomatoes add smokiness.
- For a vegan version, omit cream or use coconut cream.
