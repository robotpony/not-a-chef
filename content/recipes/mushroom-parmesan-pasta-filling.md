This is a great filling for vegetarian pasta like ravioli, tortellini, or sauced pastas like lasagne or ragu.

#### Ingredients:

- 150g shiitake mushrooms,  cleaned and trimmed then finely diced
- 150g cremini mushrooms, cleaned and trimmed then finely diced
- (optional) 1 teaspoon ground porcini powder
- 2 shallots, finely minced (~ ¼ cup)
- 3 cloves garlic, minced (~ 1 tablespoon)
- 1 teaspoon thyme leaves, minced (fresh or dried)
- Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper
- 1/2 cup dry red wine or dry sherry
- 25g grated parmesan cheese (~ ½ cup) plus more for serving

## Method:

1. Fry mushrooms in butter (or oil) and some salt until they express their water, the water evaporates, and the mushrooms start to brown (15-20 minutes). Continue frying, scraping the bottom as you go (to slow down the fond browning too much).
2. Add shallots and garlic, and soften, continuing to stir to prevent burnt fond.
3. Deglaze with wine or sherry, scraping up all of the brown bits. Add spices, stir, and taste (adjusting salt/pepper).
4. Remove from heat and let cool part way. Add parmesan, stirring in thoroughly.
5. Cool before using as a pasta filling.

If you roll the cooled filling out and freeze, you can slice it up  in 5-10g chunks depending on the size of pasta you plan to fill..
