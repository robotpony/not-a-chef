## Ingredients

- 150g (½ cup) hoisin sauce
- 60g (¼ cup) peanut butter
- 2 tablespoons rice vinegar
- 2 cloves garlic, grated
- 1 tablespoon sambal oelek
- 60 ml (¼ cup) warm water

## Method

Whisk all ingredients together until smooth. Thin with more water if needed.

## To serve

Dipping sauce for salad rolls or spring rolls. Also works alongside satay.
