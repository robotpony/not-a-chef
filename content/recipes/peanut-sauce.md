This is a simplified peanut sauce, great for salad rolls, dipping satay, and so on.

#### Ingredients:

- ½ cup (150g) hoisin sauce
- ¼ cup (60g) peanut butter
- 2 tbsp rice vinegar
- 2 cloves grated garlic
- 1 tbsp sambal
- ¼ (60ml) cup warm water
