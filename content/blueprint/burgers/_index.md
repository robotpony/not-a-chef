A burger is a stack of parts: a patty, how it's seasoned and cooked, and a build of cold crunch, something sharp, and a sauce, all on a toasted bun. Each part has its own method. A build picks one option for each part and lands on a profile: a drive-in deluxe burger is all cold crunch and sharpness, a fast-food double is salty, sweet, and rich. Every burger recipe here is one build with quantities.
