Deluxe means lettuce and tomato (it's [[White Spot burgers]] language). The build works because everything above the patty is cold, crunchy, sharp, or sour, and the patty below brings the salt, fat, and savoury depth. Every part is there because the alternative was tried and lost.

## Why these picks

- **Two onions.** Raw for sharpness, sautéed in the beef fat for sweetness. Together they do more than either alone.
- **Double-sour pickles.** The strongest sour on the burger. Make them by sprinkling citric acid over ordinary dill pickles.
- **Separate condiments.** Ketchup and mustard meet on the burger instead of in a bowl. [[Bruce's burger sauce]] or the [[In-N-Out burger spread]] are good arguments for blending them instead.
- **Rest in paper.** A few minutes wrapped in burger paper steams the bun and settles the flavours.

## How it fails

- **No cold crunch:** without the lettuce and pickle, it's a one-note burger.
- **No onion:** the onion is the most important part of a deluxe build.

## Variations

- **BC burger:** add cheddar and bacon (White Spot's name for it).
- **Blended sauce:** swap the ketchup and mustard for [[Bruce's burger sauce]].
