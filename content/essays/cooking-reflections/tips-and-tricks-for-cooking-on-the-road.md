I occasionally cook on the road while staying in corporate apartments. These kitchens are functional, but often lack basic ingredients that you don’t want to buy and leave on the road.

## How do you fry ground beef without oil?

Start it in a little simmering water instead of oil, and let it fry in its own fat once the water cooks off. The full method is in [[How do you fry ground beef without oil?]].

## How do you cook other foods without oil?

Salad dressing or water can help.

## How do you cook without salt and pepper (or other seasoning)?

Salad dressing, sausage, prepared meats all contain spices.
