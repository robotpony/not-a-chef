## Saturday, May 16th

### Fajita salads/bowls

→ [[Fajita chicken bowl]]

- fajita marinated chicken ([1](https://www.seriouseats.com/grilling-chicken-fajitas-cinco-de-mayo-mexican-recipe), [2](https://www.delish.com/cooking/recipe-ideas/a39811878/chicken-fajita-marinade-recipe/))
    - 3-4 chicken breasts _pounded to ½-inch thick, or whole (marinate longer)_
    - 100 ml lime juice + zest _(if from fresh)_
    - 80 ml olive oil
    - 10 g dark brown sugar (or white sugar, maple syrup, etc.)
    - ½ cup coarsely chopped fresh cilantro _(I finely dice the stalks for marinade)_
    - 3 cloves finely minced garlic
    - 2 ½ teaspoons whole cumin, toasted and ground
    - ½ tsp ground black pepper
    - Mexican oregano, thyme
    - cinnamon (¼ stick)
    - chilies (to taste, I sometimes use sweet paprika for colour)
    - 1 teaspoon kosher salt
    - ¼ tsp MSG

Added: cinnamon, oregano, thyme, MSG, chilies.

Toast whole spices, and grind.

Assemble marinade, and season to taste (it should be a balanced acid/sweet/salt, with some depth).

Marinate chicken (30 minutes to 12 hours), and cook (grill or broil) to medium (or ~62°C, resting to 65°C). You want consistent browning in addition to an internal temperature exceeding 62°C.

#### Lemon/lime-aid iced tea, single serving

→ [[Lemon-lime iced tea]]

- 3 heaping teaspoons sugar
- 3 dashes lime juice, 2 dashes lemon
- 500-700 ml cold tea

Shake with ice in a martini shaker, serve over ice. Balances sweet and tart, and is a nice sunny day treat.
