## Sunday, November 9th

### Goulash, v1

→ [[Goulash]]

([1](https://www.billyparisi.com/authentic-hungarian-goulash-recipe/), 2, 3, 4)

Aiming towards an easy almost American style (e.g., thicker) stew, but closer to authentic in terms of caraway and more paprika (4 tbsp+ per kg of meat, versus 1+). Skipping bacon (no need with fatty pork).

- 3 tablespoons fat (bacon, lard, fat cap, olive oil, etc.)
- 1 kg cubed stew meat (**pork shoulder**, chuck, etc.)
    - lightly flour
- 2 peeled and medium diced yellow onions
- 3 finely minced garlic cloves
- 1 seeded and medium diced red bell pepper
- 3 cored and medium diced vine ripe tomatoes (**or 1 tin**)
- 1 ½ teaspoons ground cumin or **caraway** seeds (caraway is more
- 1 (4 seems more common) tablespoon sweet Hungarian paprika
- 1 cup red wine
- 4 cups beef stock
- 2 bay leaves
- 2 peeled russet potatoes cut into 2.5 cm (1-inch) chunks
- 3 peeled and large diced or thickly sliced carrots
- optionally add 1 cup each of large diced peeled celeriac root and turnips
- salt and pepper to taste
