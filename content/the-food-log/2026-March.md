    - 1 g MSG

- Dal/mixed beans [[1](https://eagerforlife.com.au/red-lentil-and-mixed-bean-dahl/)]
- marinated pre-frozen + pressed tofu → [[Marinated pressed tofu]]
    - firm
    - freeze in container for 48-72 hours minimum
    - thaw in fridge, slice into slabs
    - marinate with
        - soy
        - rice wine (or rice wine vinegar)
        - sesame oil
        - ginger
    - bake 205°C until browned
    - freeze/fridge
    - slice for stir fry, or cube
