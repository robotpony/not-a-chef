This build is about one ingredient: onion, cooked down in the beef fat until it's sweet. Where the drive-in deluxe gets its contrast from cold crunch, this one is sweet and savoury with one soft, even texture.

## Picks

Sweet and savoury; one soft, even texture.

- **Burger patties:** Paper thin, smashed
- **Burger seasoning:** After cooking
- **Burger cooking:** Onion grilled in the beef fat alongside the patty, or smashed into it
- **Burger assembly:** A pile of cooked onion, a pickle for the only sharp note
- **Burger sauces:** Yellow mustard

```stack
bun-top     | Soft bun                  | soft
pickle      | Pickle                    | the only sour
onion-fried | Grilled onion, piled on   | sweet
patty-smash | Paper-thin patty, smashed | lacy, crisp edge
mustard     | Yellow mustard            | sharp
bun-base    | Soft bun                  | soft
```

There are two ways to get the onion there. Both are variations on the same idea.

## Grilled alongside

Slice the onion thin and cook it on the griddle next to the patty, in the fat the patty renders, until it's soft, browned, and sweet. Pile it on the patty. Cooked down further and seasoned, the same onion becomes a jam: the onion jam on a double smash burger in the [October 2026 Food Log](/the-food-log/2026-october/) was savoury and sweet.

## Smashed in

Pile thin-sliced onion on the meatball and smash the patty onion side down, so the onion cooks into the meat. It's a shortcut for grilling the onion separately: a similar effect in one step, and the onion soaks up the griddle juices instead of leaving them in the pan.

## Where it comes from

Lunch counter, 1922 railway strike.
