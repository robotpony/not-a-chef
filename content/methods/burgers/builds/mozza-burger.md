A&W Canada's bacon cheeseburger, built around mozzarella instead of cheddar. Mozzarella is mild and milky, so the sauce does the work: Mozza sauce, a creamy, tangy spread of mayo, relish, and chili sauce, goes on both buns. Bacon, lettuce, and tomato fill in the rest.

## Picks

Creamy, tangy, and mild; the sauce leads.

- **Forming burger patties:** One beef patty
- **Seasoning burgers:** Lawry's Seasoned Salt, on the griddle
- **Cooking patties:** Griddled, flipped once, mozzarella on after the flip
- **Assembling burgers:** Real mozzarella, bacon, lettuce, and tomato, on a toasted sesame seed bun
- **Sauces and condiments:** Mozza sauce on the top and bottom buns

## Where it comes from

A&W Canada sells the Mozza burger alongside the Burger Family, though it isn't one of the family. The Canadian chain has been separate from the American A&W since 1972, and its menu has drifted far from the U.S. one; the Mozza is one of the burgers only Canada has. ([A&W (Canada)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A%26W_(Canada)), Wikipedia.)

## Why these picks

- **Mozzarella.** Milder and creamier than cheddar, and it melts soft and stretchy.
- **Sauce on both buns.** The mozzarella doesn't bring much flavour, so the sauce has to. Two layers put the tang in every bite.
- **Mozza sauce.** A mayo base with sweet pickle or red relish, chili sauce, and a touch of Thousand Island dressing or sugar: creamy, sweet, and tangy, close to a Thousand Island.
- **Bacon.** The salt and smoke the mild cheese can't give.

## How it fails

- **Bland:** too little sauce, or the sauce on one bun only.
- **Slides apart:** too much sauce on a bun that wasn't toasted.
