The fresh version: nothing made ahead, just cherry tomatoes, garlic, and oil cooked down while the pasta boils.

## Picks

Bright, sweet, and garlicky, loose and oily.

- **Cooking pasta in the pan:** Long pasta, spaghetti or linguine
- **Pasta sauces:** Built fresh: cherry tomatoes burst in olive oil with garlic
- **Finishing pasta in the sauce:** Loose
- **Off the heat:** Basil, Parmesan, a drizzle of good oil
