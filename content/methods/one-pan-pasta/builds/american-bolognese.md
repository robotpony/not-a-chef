The weeknight version of a meat-sauce pasta: the sauce is already made, so dinner is the time it takes the pasta to cook.

## Picks

Rich and savoury, tight enough to cling.

- **Cooking pasta in the pan:** Short pasta, penne or rigatoni
- **Pasta sauces:** Meat sauce, from a base
- **Finishing pasta in the sauce:** Tight
- **Off the heat:** Parmesan, and basil if there is some
