Take the pan off the heat before the finishing notes go in. Cheese turns grainy and stringy over a flame, butter and good oil break, and herbs go dark. Off the heat they melt into the sauce instead. Then plate it straight away, since the sauce keeps tightening as it sits.

## Options

| Option | Pick it when | Changes |
|---|---|---|
| Cheese and butter | A tomato or meat sauce | Parmesan stirred in, more on top |
| Oil and herbs | A fresh sauce | Good olive oil and torn basil |
| Lemon and chili | It needs brightening | Zest and juice, chili flakes |

## Notes 

- Cheese tends to break around 54°C to 71°C (130°F to 160°F). Off the heat, you can control the temperature better.
