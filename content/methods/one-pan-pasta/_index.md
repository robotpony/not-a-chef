---
title: One-pan pasta
description: "Pasta cooked in the pan it's sauced in, in just enough water to cover, so the water turns starchy and finishes the sauce the way a restaurant's pasta water does."
---
One-pan pasta is how a restaurant finishes a pasta dish, done in a single pan at home. On the line, pasta comes out of a pot that's been boiling pasta all day and goes into a pan of sauce with a splash of that water. The water is cloudy with starch, and it tightens the sauce until it clings to the pasta. At home the pot water is clean, so the pasta cooks in the sauce pan itself, in just enough water to cover it, and that little bit of water gets as starchy as the restaurant's. It saves a pot, and the sauce is better for it. Every one-pan pasta here is a build: the same four parts with a different sauce.
