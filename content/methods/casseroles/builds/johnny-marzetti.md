Pasta, ground beef, and tomato sauce, baked under cheese. It's a baked pasta made for feeding a crowd cheaply, and it was a school cafeteria staple in central Ohio.

## Picks

Savoury, tomatoey, and cheesy.

- **The starch:** Elbow macaroni or other short pasta, mixed through
- **The filling:** Ground beef, onions, and sometimes mushrooms
- **The sauce:** Tomato sauce, or tomato soup
- **The top and the bake:** Grated cheese

## Where it comes from

Local legend ties it to Marzetti's, a Columbus, Ohio, restaurant opened in 1896, but the Marzetti company has found no evidence for that. The earliest dated recipe is from the *Columbus Dispatch* in 1953, and by the 1970s it was common in Columbus-area school cafeterias ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johnny_Marzetti)).
