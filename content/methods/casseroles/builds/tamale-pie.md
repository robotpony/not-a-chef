Seasoned meat or chili baked under a cornmeal crust. The cornmeal is the starch, and it plays the part a tamale's masa does.

## Picks

Savoury and a little spicy, under a soft, golden crust.

- **The starch:** Cornmeal dough or batter, on top, and sometimes lining the dish
- **The filling:** Ground beef, chicken, or leftover chili, with beans or corn
- **The sauce:** Tomatoes or salsa
- **The top and the bake:** The cornmeal crust, sometimes with cheese

## Where it comes from

Tamale pie is from the United States around the turn of the 20th century, in the cooking of the Southwest. The first recipe by that name was published in 1911; earlier ones from Austin (1899) and Los Angeles (1905) used a wheat crust, or cornmeal above and below ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tamale_pie)).
