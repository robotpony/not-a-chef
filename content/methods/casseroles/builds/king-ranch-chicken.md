Shredded chicken, peppers, onion, and tomatoes with green chiles in a creamy sauce, layered with corn tortillas and baked under cheese. It's a layered casserole, closer to lasagne than most, but built from what's in the pantry.

## Picks

Creamy, mildly spicy, and cheesy.

- **The starch:** Corn tortillas or tortilla chips, layered
- **The filling:** Shredded chicken, bell peppers, and onion
- **The sauce:** Cream of mushroom and cream of chicken soup, with tomatoes and green chiles
- **The top and the bake:** Shredded cheese

## Where it comes from

Its origin is unknown, and it has no known connection to the King Ranch it's named for. It probably came about after the Second World War, when canned-food casseroles took off, and it was popular in women's clubs in South and West Texas in the 1950s ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_Ranch_chicken)).
