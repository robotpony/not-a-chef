Macaroni in a cream or cheese sauce with cheese on top, baked until it bubbles. It's the baked pasta with the longest written history, and still the one most people make.

## Picks

Rich, salty, and creamy, under a browned top.

- **The starch:** Macaroni, mixed through
- **The filling:** None, or bacon, jalapeños, or vegetables
- **The sauce:** A cheese sauce (mornay), or cream thickened with butter and flour
- **The top and the bake:** Grated cheese, or buttered crumbs

## Where it comes from

Elizabeth Raffald's *The Experienced English Housekeeper* (1769) has macaroni in cream thickened with butter and flour, with Parmesan over the top. In the United States, Thomas Jefferson served "a pie called macaroni" at a state dinner in 1802, and Mary Randolph's *The Virginia House-Wife* (1824) bakes macaroni, cheese, and butter in a hot oven ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Macaroni_and_cheese)).
