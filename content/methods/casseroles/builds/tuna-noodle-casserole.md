Canned tuna and egg noodles in cream of mushroom soup, often with peas, under something crunchy. Everything in it keeps in the cupboard, which was the point.

## Picks

Creamy, salty, and soft, with a crunchy top.

- **The starch:** Egg noodles, mixed through
- **The filling:** Canned tuna and peas
- **The sauce:** Cream of mushroom soup
- **The top and the bake:** Breadcrumbs, fried onions, crushed potato chips, or corn flakes

## Where it comes from

Casseroles took off in North American homes in the 1950s, and in 1952 Campbell's published a book of canned-soup recipes that included tuna casserole ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tuna_casserole)). Campbell's had been making condensed cream of mushroom soup since 1934 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cream_of_mushroom_soup)).
