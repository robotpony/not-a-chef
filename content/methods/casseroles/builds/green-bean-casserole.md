Green beans in mushroom soup and milk, baked under crisp fried onions. It's a casserole with no starch: the fried onions on top do the job a crust would.

## Picks

Creamy and savoury, with a crunchy top.

- **The starch:** None
- **The filling:** Green beans
- **The sauce:** Cream of mushroom soup and milk, with soy sauce and pepper
- **The top and the bake:** Crisp fried onions, some mixed in and the rest on top

## Where it comes from

Dorcas Reilly created it in 1955 at Campbell's, for an Associated Press feature on quick dishes from ingredients most kitchens had on hand. It became a Thanksgiving staple in the 1960s, once the recipe was printed on the soup can ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green_bean_casserole)).
