The whole turkey dinner, stacked in the order you'd eat it off a fork. It's the best use of the leftovers, because everything in it was made to go together the night before.

## Picks

Rich, savoury, and soft, under a browned top.

- **The starch:** Stuffing in the middle, mashed potatoes on top
- **The filling:** Leftover turkey and the roasted vegetables
- **The sauce:** The turkey gravy from dinner
- **The top and the bake:** Mash ridged with a fork, browned under the broiler

```stack
mash        | Mashed potatoes       | lid, browned
stuffing    | Stuffing              | savoury, soaks the gravy
veg-roasted | Roasted vegetables    | sweet
meat-chunks | Turkey, in bite-sized pieces | savoury
gravy       | Turkey gravy          | binds
caption: Thanksgiving casserole, top to bottom
```

## Why these picks

- **Gravy at the bottom.** It soaks up into the turkey and stuffing as it bakes, so nothing dries out.
- **Stuffing under the mash.** It keeps its own layer and catches the gravy; on top, it would crisp instead, which also works.

## How it fails

- **Dry:** not enough gravy. Turkey and stuffing both drink it. Make extra gravy on the day, or stretch it with stock.
- **Cold in the middle:** it's a deep dish of cold leftovers. Check the centre reaches 74°C (165°F).

## Variations

- **Cranberry:** a thin layer of cranberry sauce over the turkey.
- **Stuffing on top:** skip the mash and let the stuffing crisp.
- **[[Turkey tetrazzini]]:** the turkey in a sherry cream sauce, over spaghetti.
