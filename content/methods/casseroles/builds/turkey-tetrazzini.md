Diced poultry and mushrooms in a cream sauce with sherry, mixed with spaghetti and baked under crumbs or cheese. It began as a hotel dish, and became a good use for leftover turkey.

## Picks

Creamy, savoury, and a little boozy.

- **The starch:** Spaghetti, linguine, or egg noodles, mixed through
- **The filling:** Leftover turkey or chicken and mushrooms
- **The sauce:** Butter, cream, and cheese, with sherry or white wine
- **The top and the bake:** Breadcrumbs or Parmesan

## Where it comes from

It's named for the opera singer Luisa Tetrazzini, around the time of her American debut in 1905. Who made it first is disputed: the Palace Hotel in San Francisco, the Knickerbocker Hotel in New York, and Escoffier are all credited ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tetrazzini)).
