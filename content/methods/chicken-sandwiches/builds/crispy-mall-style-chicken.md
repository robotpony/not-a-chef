The sandwich from a one-counter shop in a mall food court, the one I've been chasing ever since. Everything about it was quiet except the crust: dark, thick, and gritty with cornmeal, on a piece of chicken you could tell had been a breast that morning. The toppings stayed out of its way.

The story is in [[Chicken sandwich, crispy, mall style]].

## Picks

All crust, with quiet toppings.

- **Chicken sandwich patties:** Whole breast, not pressed into a perfect patty
- **Breading and frying chicken patties:** Medium-grind cornmeal cut with flour, fried dark
- **Assembling chicken sandwiches:** Lettuce and maybe a slice of tomato, on a kaiser with a crusty outside and a chewy, airy inside
- **Chicken sandwich sauces:** Mayo

```stack
bun-top + mayo       | Kaiser, mayo          | chewy, rich
lettuce              | Lettuce               | cold crunch
tomato               | Tomato, maybe         | juicy
patty-chicken-breast | Cornmeal-fried breast | crunch, salt, fat
bun-base             | Kaiser                | chew, barrier
caption: Crispy, mall-style chicken sandwich, top to bottom
```

## Why these picks

- **Nothing competes with the crust.** Mayo, lettuce, and maybe tomato. The crust is the whole point.
- **A kaiser.** Chewy enough to hold up to a thick, crunchy crust without being squashed by it.
- **Cut with flour.** Straight cornmeal doesn't hold together; some flour makes it stick.

## How it fails

- **Pale crust:** not fried long enough. It wants to be dark.
- **Too much going on:** a full deluxe build buries the crust. That's [[The BCS]].
