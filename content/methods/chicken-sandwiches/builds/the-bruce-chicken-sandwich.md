The chicken sandwich I make at home. It's the crunchy cornmeal crust I've been chasing since the mall food court, built like [[The Bruce burger]]: everything cold, sharp, and sour stacked over a hot patty, with mayo and mustard kept apart.

It's had three versions, below.

## Picks

A dark, crunchy crust against cold crunch, sharp onion, and sour pickles.

- **Chicken sandwich patties:** Whole breast, pounded to 1 cm, buttermilk soak
- **Breading and frying chicken patties:** Cornmeal dredge, shallow-fried
- **Assembling chicken sandwiches:** Mayo on both buns, shredded lettuce, thin tomato, thin-sliced raw red onion, sour pickles, on a toasted bun
- **Chicken sandwich sauces:** Mayo and mustard, kept separate

```stack
bun-top + mayo   | Toasted bun, mayo   | soft, rich
lettuce-shredded | Shredded lettuce    | cold crunch
tomato           | Thin tomato         | juicy, acid
onion-raw        | Raw red onion       | sharp
pickle           | Sour pickles        | sour
mustard          | Mustard             | sharp
patty-chicken-breast    | Cornmeal-fried breast | crunch, salt, fat
bun-base + mayo  | Toasted bun, mayo   | rich, barrier
caption: The Bruce chicken sandwich, top to bottom
```

## Iterations

| Year | Version | What changed |
|---|---|---|
| 1991 | Behind the counter | A fast-food breaded patty, built [[The Whopper\|Whopper]] style: onion, pickle, tomato, ketchup, the works (see [[The impossible quest for the perfect chicken burger]]). |
| 2015 | The home version | A homemade breaded patty ([[Pan-fried chicken burger]]), with mustard in place of the ketchup, and extra pickles and onion. |
| 2026 | The cornmeal patty | A cornmeal-fried breast ([[Cornmeal fried chicken]]), the crust from the mall food court. The picks above are this version. |

## Why these picks

- **Cornmeal crust.** The darkest, crunchiest coating, and the closest I've come to the mall sandwich. It holds up under a full build.
- **Mayo on both buns.** The fat the breast doesn't have, and a barrier that keeps the bottom crust dry.
- **Mustard on the patty.** Sharpness right against the crust, where it cuts the fried fat.
- **Red onion and sour pickles.** The same job they do on the burger: something sharp and something sour, so the third bite is as good as the first.

## How it fails

- **Soft crust:** it waited on paper towels, or the tomato sat on it. Hold the patty on a rack and build right before eating.
- **One-note:** the onion or pickles are missing.

## Variations

- **Mall style:** mayo, lettuce, and tomato only, on a kaiser. The crust carries it (see [[Chicken sandwich, crispy, mall style]]).
- **Ground patty:** swap the breast for a double-coated [[Pan-fried chicken burger]] patty.
