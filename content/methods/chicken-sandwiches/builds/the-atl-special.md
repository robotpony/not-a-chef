A sweet, hot, and crunchy build on the cornmeal-fried breast. Sweet pickles and maple bring the sweetness, the chilies and mustard bring the heat and sharpness, and the slaw is the cold crunch.

## Picks

Sweet and hot over a crunchy crust, with cold slaw.

- **Chicken sandwich patties:** Whole breast, pounded to 1 cm, buttermilk soak
- **Breading and frying chicken patties:** Cornmeal dredge, shallow-fried
- **Assembling chicken sandwiches:** Sweet pickles and slaw, on a toasted bun
- **Chicken sandwich sauces:** Spicy maple mustard (mayo, mustard, maple syrup, ground hot chilies), drizzled over the patty

## Why these picks

- **A drizzle, not a spread.** Thin enough to season the crust without soaking it. The mayo in it carries the fat a bun spread would.
- **Slaw for the cold crunch.** It does the lettuce's job with more crunch and some acid. It goes on top, away from the crust.
- **Sweet pickles.** Sweet and sour at once, against the heat.

## How it fails

- **Soggy:** the slaw sat on the sandwich, or was too wet. Drain it and build right before eating.
- **Cloying:** too much maple. Add more mustard or chili.

## Variations

- **Honey mustard:** swap the maple for honey.
- **Ground patty:** swap the breast for a [[Pan-fried chicken burger]] patty.
