Fresh ginger, turmeric, and lime in coconut water and orange juice, with a pinch of salt. It's alcohol-free as written; the variations make it sparkling, creamy, or a cocktail.

## Ingredients

- 1 tsp fresh ginger, frozen and grated
- ½ tsp fresh turmeric, frozen and grated, or ¼ tsp ground turmeric
- 1 lime, zested and juiced
- 120 ml (½ cup) sweetened coconut water, with pulp
- 120 ml (½ cup) orange juice, or 1 orange, squeezed
- A pinch of salt
- Ice

## Method

**Grate the roots.** Freeze the ginger and turmeric for at least 30 minutes, then grate them on a Microplane. Frozen, they grate to a fine snow instead of stringy pulp.

**Mix.** Put the ginger, turmeric, lime zest, lime juice, and salt in a glass and stir until the salt dissolves. Add the coconut water and orange juice and stir.

**Serve.** Add ice and stir once more.

## Variations

**Sparkling.** Use half the coconut water and top the glass with sparkling water (San Pellegrino or Perrier).

**Creamy.** Swap 60 ml (¼ cup) of the coconut water for coconut milk, and shake it all with ice so the coconut milk blends in.

**Cocktail.** Add 45 ml (1½ oz) blanco tequila or white rum.

## Notes

- Fresh turmeric stains hands, boards, and counters. Grate it over the glass.
