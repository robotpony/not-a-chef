A tequila espresso cocktail with amaretto, coffee liqueur, and Irish cream, dusted with cocoa.

## Mechanic

Shake it hard with **fresh, hot espresso**. The crema and the Irish cream whip into a foam cap as it chills, and that foam is what holds the cocoa on top. Day-old or cold coffee shakes up flat.

## Ingredients

- 30 ml (1 oz) blanco tequila
- 15 ml (½ oz) amaretto
- 15 ml (½ oz) Caffè Borghetti, or another coffee liqueur
- 15 ml (½ oz) Irish cream liqueur, or [[Homemade Baileys Irish cream]]
- 30 ml (1 oz) espresso, freshly pulled
- Sweetened cocoa powder, for dusting
- Ice

## Method

**Chill the glass.** Put a coupe or martini glass in the freezer, or fill it with ice water while you pull the espresso.

**Shake.** Fill a shaker with ice. Add the tequila, amaretto, coffee liqueur, Irish cream, and espresso. Shake hard until the shaker is frosted, about 15 seconds.

**Strain and dust.** Empty the glass and strain the drink into it. Let the foam settle for a few seconds, then dust it lightly with cocoa through a small sieve.
