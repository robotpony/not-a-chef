Also called a wang wang. This is how it's served on Holland America Line ships.

## Mechanic

The drink is **eight spirits in equal ¼ oz (7.5 ml) parts**, 60 ml (2 oz) of liquor in all, so it's as strong as a double. Pineapple and orange juice hide almost all of it, so it drinks far lighter than it is.

## Ingredients

- 7.5 ml (¼ oz) vodka
- 7.5 ml (¼ oz) gin
- 7.5 ml (¼ oz) white rum
- 7.5 ml (¼ oz) Scotch
- 7.5 ml (¼ oz) bourbon
- 7.5 ml (¼ oz) tequila
- 7.5 ml (¼ oz) triple sec
- 7.5 ml (¼ oz) brandy
- pineapple juice, to top up
- orange juice, to top up
- a splash of grenadine
- ice

## Method

**Build the spirits.** Fill a tall glass with ice and pour in all eight spirits.

**Top up.** Fill the glass with roughly equal parts pineapple and orange juice, and stir.

**Finish.** Add a splash of grenadine last, without stirring, so it sinks to the bottom.
