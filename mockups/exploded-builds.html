Exploded build diagrams

The layer bars in methods-pages.html were abstract, and full-width bars don't look like a burger. This draws a build the way a technical manual draws an assembly: each layer as the thing itself, seen from a three-quarter angle, pulled apart vertically, with leader lines to its name and its role.

Round 2 drew the same shapes in the formula icons' style. Round 4 (2026-10-11) keeps that style and its two fills and adds texture as strokes, after Bruce's references (a patent drawing, an engraving, a colouring book): sesame seeds as open ovals over the dome, stipple on cut bread and patty sides, a craggy patty edge, a seared top on griddled patties and edge-to-edge grill marks on grilled ones (two keys, -grilled), veined and ruffled lettuce, tomato chambers with seeds, onion as loose rings, crinkle-cut pickle chips with ridges and a seed ring, bacon as three rippled strips laid across the patty's ellipse, and a hatched cheese slice draped at the corners. Ketchup and mustard are layered swirls sized by share (ketchup the large one, 2/3, as wide as the patty; mustard the small one on top, 1/3, in the bun's fill); mayo and burger sauce are spreads, so the two never look alike. Sections 2 to 6 show round 4; round 3 is a click away in the top bar.

8 · Recommendation and open questions

Use the exploded view for builds, in the formula style, labelled on build pages (labels on the right, decided 2026-10-10) and compact on cards. It reads as a burger, keeps its proportions, and now sits in the same family as the formula icons: one ink line, two fills, theme tokens, so dark mode follows without special colours.

  • What the formula style gives up. Lettuce, tomato, and pickles are told apart by shape alone, not green and red. The shapes carry it at full size; check the compact card version in particular.
  • How much detail? Round 4: moderate, not realistic. Texture is strokes and stipple on top of the two fills; no shading fills, no colour. Check the compact card size in particular, where stipple can go grey.
  • Swirls and spreads. A swirl's size is its share of the pair, not its exact ratio: 2/3 and 1/3 even when the recipe says 5:1. The label carries the number.
  • Draw by hand or generate? Generated here from a few shape functions (disc, dome, wavy disc, slab). As SVG files in a kit they'd be drawn once each and stacked by the render hook.
  • Colour version. If it's ever wanted, it needs fixed food colours (--f-*), since theme tokens turn a patty pink in dark mode.
  • Assembled view: not needed for now.
