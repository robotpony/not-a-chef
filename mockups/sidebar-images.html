Photos in the sidebar

Images come out of the recipe or essay body and into a Photos module in the sidebar: thumbnails in the rail, a pop-over viewer on click. Real pages and the real photos already on the site. Click any thumbnail; arrow keys step through, Esc closes.

How it behaves

  • Rail placement. Photos sits right under the Date rows (recipes: under the meta rail), above Notes and "On this page". It's the one visual thing in the rail, so it goes where the eye lands first.
  • Grid by count. 1 photo: full rail width at 4:3 with its caption. 2 or 4: two columns. 3, 5, 6: three columns of squares, no captions. Past 6, the sixth tile shows +N and opens the viewer there.
  • Crops only in the rail. Thumbnails are object-fit: cover, so the 9:16 burger phone shot and the 4:3 soup both fit the grid. The viewer always shows the whole photo, uncropped.
  • Phone. The reading rail falls below the article and the recipe rail starts collapsed, so a photo in either would be out of sight. On phones the module moves up into the page head as a strip of 64px thumbnails under the tags.
  • Viewer. A native <dialog>: dark backdrop in both themes, whole image fitted to the screen, caption from the alt text, "N / M" counter, prev/next, Esc or backdrop click to close, swipe on touch. Focus returns to the thumbnail that opened it.
  • Marker in text (optional). A small "Photo" button where the image used to be. It matters for the Food Log, where a photo belongs to a specific entry; the viewer's "From" line covers the same need if the marker feels like clutter.

How it would build

  • Authoring stays the same. Keep writing ![alt](/images/…) in Obsidian, so the vault preview still shows photos inline.
  • A render-image hook (themes/fugu/layouts/_default/_markup/render-image.html, new) wraps each image in a <figure data-sidebar-photo> with an id and records the nearest heading for the "From" line.
  • automagic-sidebar.js already moves Notes, Mechanic, and friends into data-sidebar-slot boxes. A photos slot gets the same treatment: gather the figures, build the grid, leave the marker behind. Without JS, photos stay inline where they were written.
  • Print hides the sidebar, so the inline figures should stay in the DOM, hidden on screen and shown in print.
  • Open question: thumbnail weight. Images live in static/, which Hugo can't resize; the soup photo is 4032×3024. Real thumbnails need the images moved to assets/ (or page bundles) so the hook can call .Fill "400x400". Until then the rail loads full-size files.
  • Open question: lead photo on cards. Once a recipe has photos, the first one could also go on its recipe card. Out of scope here.
