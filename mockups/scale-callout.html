Scaling callout

The gear beside a recipe's Ingredients heading opens the scale and units controls. Today it opens a plain box set a little apart from the gear: a wide slider, then three unit buttons that don't fit on one line, so "Imperial" wraps onto a line by itself.

The proposal: a callout with a small ^ pointer up to the gear, so it reads as coming from that button; a tidier slider with its whole-number steps marked; and units as one drop list.

§2

Proposed

Same surface, border and shadow family as the glossary popover, so the site has one popover look. The pointer's tip sits under the gear's centre. Each control gets one row: label and current value on top, the control under it. Live: drag the slider and change the list.

§3

Units: drop list or segments

Both fix the wrap. The drop list is the cleaner of the two and leaves room if Kelvin (the site's joke option, off by default) is ever turned on. A segmented control shows all three at once, one tap to switch, but a fourth option wouldn't fit.

§4

States & phone

The drop list's open state is the browser's own: a menu on desktop, a picker wheel or sheet on phones, which is a big part of why a native list beats custom buttons here. Keyboard: Tab from the gear goes slider → list, arrow keys work on both, Escape closes the callout.

§6

Open questions

1

Drop list or segments? The drop list (A) is recommended, as you suggested; segments (B) is one tap but can't take a fourth option.

2

Yield line under the slider? "Serves 3–4 · 6 patties" updating as you drag, from the recipe's servings and portions. The sidebar's Serves row already scales; this puts the answer next to the control. Hidden on recipes with neither field.

3

Tick labels 1–5? They make half steps readable (the thumb between 1 and 2 is 1.5×) at the cost of one small line.

4

One popover look site-wide? If this lands, the glossary popover could take the pointer too.
