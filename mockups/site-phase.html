Plant-based, the American split, the build number

Three picks for you: how the plant-based chip reads on a card, which recipes carry it, and where each American recipe goes. The build number is built; it's here for a look.

1 · Plant-based chip on a card

3 · Which recipes are plant-based

"Plant-forward," not "vegan": a proposed rule is a vegetarian or vegan main, soup, side, salad, or base, but not sauces, dressings, drinks, desserts, or spice pastes. The last few are not vegetarian but lean on vegetables; check the ones you want. Untick anything that doesn't belong.

4 · The American split

American stays for dishes that really are American (burgers, BBQ, Buffalo, ranch, diner baking). The two Southern ones get a region. Everyday cooking with no real cuisine loses the field (cuisine is optional, FORMAT.md); a few family recipes that read as Canadian move there. Change any row; the list at the bottom is what goes to the content agent.

6 · Telling the two principles apart

Built, the leaf and WTF read as one green pair. There's no sixth hue, so these vary the chip's weight, not its colour. The phone card (165px) shows what survives when the row is full.
