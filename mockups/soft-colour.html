Softer colour

A thinking mock for PLAN.md's Design v2 item "Soften the colour scheme". The same cards and recipe excerpt three times, each with its own tokens. The five hues stay in every column; what changes is how loud they are and how many get a filled shape.

B's tokens are a first guess, not tuned: saturation pulled roughly a third toward the neutrals, ink a step lighter, fills nearer the surface. C uses the same tokens as B and changes components, not colours. The mixer below takes each change separately.

What to look for

  • Card rows: in A, a test kitchen card with a cuisine shows three filled hues before the title. Does B quiet that enough, or does it take C's text-only chips?
  • Accent: A's rust on stat numbers, ratios, links, and quantity washes makes it everywhere. C keeps it for things that teach or click (ratios, links, quantities) and drops it from stat numbers.
  • Draft banner: C turns the filled blue box into two hairlines, Smitten Kitchen's rules instead of boxes.
  • Dark mode: A's dark accents are bright pastels; B pulls them down the most, since that's where the loudness shows.
  • Contrast: B's chip text and ink-faint need a WCAG check before anything lands; the softer yellow is the one at risk.
