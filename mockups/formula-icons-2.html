Formula icons, round 2

Fifteen new icons for the formula kit, so the Mechanics that state a ratio but need ingredients the kit can't draw yet can get a diagram: dressings and marinades, soy braises, burger sauces, lentils, fruit, spice blends, and the stock and reverse cooking sequences.

Same rules as the first kit (STYLE.md, Formula diagram): a 48-unit grid, 2-unit round strokes in currentColor, one --accent-soft fill on the body, --surface on lids and tops. Toggle the theme to check dark mode.

04

Questions

Q1

One acid icon or two? Lemon for citrus, the cruet for vinegar, mirin, and sake. Mirin on a vinegar bottle is a stretch; a label carries it, but a third "bottle" icon is the alternative.

Q2

MSG. Drawn with the salt shaker and its own label (McDouble). A separate icon would only serve one recipe today.

Q3

Nuts. Granola bars (dates : oats : nuts, 1:2.5:1) need a nut icon. Left out until a second recipe needs one.

Q4

Oven in the reverse sear. The live page uses the clock for the gentle phase; the oven says "low oven" more plainly. Swap it once the oven icon lands?

Q5

Classic French dressing. Its Mechanic states the 1:1 it uses and the 3:1 it isn't. Drawing the 1:1 only.
