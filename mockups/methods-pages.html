Methods and blueprint pages

The concept pages for methods (docs/METHODS.md): the methods list, a blueprint's page, and how a method page and its card show where they belong. They reuse the principle page's parts (header, definition callout, card grid, reference list) in the accent colour, plus one new component, the ordered parts list.

Content is illustrative: only reverse cooking and food ratios exist as methods today, and none of the burger parts are written yet.

1 · Methods page /tags/method/

Blueprints first, each with its parts in order, so the page reads as a map. Methods that belong to no blueprint (techniques and ratios) follow as cards. Written parts link; unwritten ones are listed faintly, so a blueprint shows its whole shape from the start.

4 · A method page's header and footer

A part's method. The tags row leads with the method chip, then a blueprint chip naming the part, which replaces the plain Blowfish badge hidden today. The same blueprint chip (without the part) goes on burger recipe and story pages. The part's options are a table in its body (## Options), not pages. At the foot, previous and next part in the blueprint's order.

7 · Open choices

These are proposals, not decisions. Each card says what the mock shows and the main alternative. Only the last one is settled.

Open

1 · Unwritten parts

Proposed: Every part of a blueprint is listed, including ones with no method yet, shown faint with "Not written yet". The page shows the blueprint's whole shape from day one.

Alternative: List only the parts that have a method, plus a count ("2 of 6 parts"). Tidier, but the blueprint looks incomplete in a way readers can't see.

Open

2 · Blueprint chip style

Proposed: An accent outline chip ("Burgers · Patty construction"), not filled, so it sits below the filled Method and cuisine chips and reads as "belongs to".

Alternative: A filled chip like the others, or a plain text link under the title ("Part of the Burgers blueprint").

Open

3 · Order on a blueprint page

Proposed: Builds first (cards, then the comparison table), then parts, then recipes, then stories. People think in builds ("a deluxe").

Alternative: Parts first, then builds. Reads more like a reference: how a burger is made, then the ways to combine it.

Open

4 · Methods page address

Proposed: Keep the methods list at /tags/method/, the tag's own page, like Win the fridge at /tags/win-the-fridge/. No new section.

Alternative: A dedicated /methods/ page (or an alias to it), which is a nicer URL to share and link from the nav.

Open

5 · Order on the methods page

Proposed: Blueprints first (each with its parts listed), then standalone techniques and ratios as cards.

Alternative: Everything as one grid of cards, blueprints mixed in.

Open

6 · Callout label icon

Proposed: The definition callout's label carries the kind's icon (fan-out for methods and blueprints; the principle and test kitchen icons for theirs).

Alternative: No icon in the label, as on the Win the fridge page today.

Open

7 · Build profile style

Proposed: The free-text profile sits under a build's title as an italic line with an accent rule, the same on its card, its page, and the comparison table.

Alternative: Plain body text, like a card's intro.

Open

8 · Part navigation

Proposed: At the foot of a method in a blueprint: back to the blueprint, and next part in its order, replacing the usual date-ordered previous/next.

Alternative: Keep the usual previous/next, and rely on the blueprint page for order.

Decided

9 · Options aren't pages

Proposed: Decided 2026-10-10: a part's options (thick / thin / paper thin) are a table in the part's method, shown as pills on the blueprint page.
