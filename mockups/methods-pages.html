Methods and blueprint pages

The concept pages for methods (docs/METHODS.md): the methods list, a blueprint's page, and how a method page and its card show where they belong. They reuse the principle page's parts (header, definition callout, card grid, reference list) in the accent colour, plus one new component, the ordered parts list.

Content is illustrative: only reverse cooking and food ratios exist as methods today, and none of the burger parts are written yet.

1 · Methods page /tags/method/

Blueprints first, each with its parts in order, so the page reads as a map. Methods that belong to no blueprint (techniques and ratios) follow as cards. Written parts link; unwritten ones are listed faintly, so a blueprint shows its whole shape from the start.

6 · Decisions in this mock

Unwritten parts are shown

A blueprint lists all of its parts, with the unwritten ones faint ("Not written yet"), so it shows its whole shape from the first method. The alternative is to list only written parts and a count.

The callout label takes the kind's icon

"Method" and "Blueprint" carry the fan-out; principle and test kitchen would carry theirs, so the shared callout always says which kind it is.

Blueprint chip: accent outline

Not filled, so it ranks below the method chip and the cuisine chip, and reads as "belongs to" rather than "is". The part name follows in muted text on method pages only.

No new page for the methods list

It stays at /tags/method/, a term page with a kind, like Win the fridge. A /methods/ alias can come later if the URL matters.

Blueprints before cards

The methods page leads with blueprints because they're the map; standalone techniques and ratios follow. With many blueprints, this becomes a grid of blueprint blocks.

Part navigation

Previous and next part in the blueprint's order, at the foot of a method page, in place of the date-ordered article pagination.
