Principle page: the same sidebar as the methods list

Follows methods-list-sidebar.html (decided: sidebar, intro text instead of a callout, builds rail). The win-the-fridge tag page is the other concept page that opens with a definition, and it should match, so concept pages share one frame with recipes and essays.

1 · Review of the page as built

What works: a full-width grid of cards gives it a right edge, so it never felt as lopsided as the methods list did. The green "principle" in the title and the green callout make the kind clear.

What doesn't: the callout stops at 72ch and leaves about a quarter of the width empty beside it. The search box sits under "Recipes that follow this principle" but searches the whole site (219 recipes), so it reads as filtering five cards when it doesn't. "Reference & further reading" sits at the bottom, under the pagination once there are more than 16 recipes. And the full essay is reachable only from the callout's link.

With the sidebar: search goes where it is on every recipe and essay page (top of the sidebar), so it stops looking like a filter. The counts and the chip as it appears on cards ("On cards") explain the WTF mark you see in the grid. Further reading moves up beside the definition, with the full essay first. The definition becomes the page's intro, as on the methods list. The cost: the grid drops from four cards across to two or three.

2 · Mock

Layout switches between the page as built and the sidebar version. Definition and Cards apply to the sidebar version: three across needs the card minimum dropped from 240px to 200px (the related-recipes grid already uses 200px).

Win the fridge principle

Definition

Win the fridge is the practice of using every scrap of food before it goes to waste — working through the fridge, freezer, and pantry regularly instead of letting things quietly expire. It’s a frugal habit and an ethical one: food waste is avoidable, and being well-fed is a privilege worth not squandering.

It extends nose-to-tail and root-to-stem thinking to everyday cooking — every cut from a butchered animal, every part of a vegetable (stalks, leaves, peels), and anything else that would otherwise be thrown out but is still delicious and nourishing.

3 · Choices

Recommendation

Sidebar, intro, three across. The same frame and intro as the methods list. Three across at 200px keeps the grid feeling like a grid; two across makes the cards rows of two, and a principle with 16 recipes on a page runs eight rows deep. The cost at 200px: a card with three times (prep, cook, serves) wraps its last value, as on Lazy tomato soup here; the meta row would need a tighter gap or to drop serves at that width.

  • The green stays: "principle" in the title, the "Read the full essay →" link, and the chip in the sidebar. Without the callout there's no green box, but the title already names the kind.
  • Further reading lists the full essay first (it's excluded from the list today because the callout links it), then everything else on the tag that isn't a recipe. The link under the intro stays too, since that's where you finish reading the definition.
  • Search is the sidebar's site-wide box, the same as on recipe pages. The per-page "Search 219 recipes" box goes.
  • Scope: only principle terms (principle: true) change. Plain tag and cuisine pages are recipe listings and stay full width, like the recipes list. The rule: concept pages that open with a definition (the methods list, principles) get the sidebar; listings don't. The blueprint page is left alone for now: its parts already have their own right-hand column of recipes.
  • Where it lands: the template side is Fugu's _default/term.html principle branch (fugu-agent), and can share the methods list's sidebar pieces. The card minimum is this site's custom.css.
