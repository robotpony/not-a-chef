What changed

  • Tags: two slots, then +N. Cuisine takes a slot when a recipe has one, and a tag that repeats the cuisine ("thai" next to Thai) is skipped. The row never wraps; a long tag truncates instead.
  • Intro: the recipe's first paragraph (the text before the first ##), clamped to 3 lines. 141 of 228 recipes have one today. A recipe without one just gets a shorter card (see the salsa card in 03).
  • Stats: one inline line instead of a stacked label/value grid, roughly a third of the height. Values are bold --accent-strong against faint labels, so the line reads apart from the intro above it. Times are shortened for the card only ("35 min" becomes "35m", "8-10 hours" becomes "8-10h"). Serves truncates with an ellipsis when it's free text.
  • Flags removed: Mechanic/Variations are gone from the card. They still show on the recipe page itself.
