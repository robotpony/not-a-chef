The options

A · As built

Mechanic is the third block in the sidebar, set in small grey italic (.recipe-sidebar-section .mechanic), below search and the facts. On a phone it's inside Recipe notes, which starts closed and opens with the search box first.

B · Callout in the method Recommended

The Mechanic leaves the sidebar and sits above Ingredients as the ratified Mechanic callout (STYLE.md Components: an --accent left border), in roman body text with the ratio in bold, and its formula under it. It's the first thing you read on desktop and on a phone, and the sidebar gets shorter by its height. On a phone, search moves to the end of Recipe notes, so the notes open on the facts.

C · Sidebar, first

The Mechanic stays beside the recipe but leads it, above search and the facts, in roman text. On a phone it sits above the Recipe notes toggle, outside the fold. Less change to the article column, but on desktop the sidebar is still as long as it is now, and the eye still starts on the method.

Measured

Desktop, current page and theme. "Overhang" is how far the sidebar runs past the end of the article column (negative when the article is longer). It's the empty left column at the bottom of Chai.

Quieter sidebar

With the Mechanic gone, or moved to the top, what's left is reference: facts, To serve, Notes, Variations. The "quieter" switch sets that text in --ink-muted at .82rem with tighter section gaps, so it reads as notes beside the recipe, not a second column competing with it. Headings, the meta labels and links stay as they are.
