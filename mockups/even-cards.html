Even cards

Cards in a row are the same height, but what's inside them lands at different heights. A formula row follows its intro, so it floats up when the intro is short. A card with no times ends without its rule. A short burger stack leaves a gap above it. These are the real recipe list and burgers blueprint, restyled per option.

Recipe cards

A · As built

The formula row follows the intro, so it sits wherever the intro ends: 8px above the rule on most cards, 17px on Mozza burger sauce, whose intro is a line short, with the blank under the formula instead of above it. In a row with two formulas and uneven intros, they'd sit at different heights. A card with no times (Quick relish) has no stats line, so its rule is missing and it ends in blank space.

B · Formula on a shared line Recommended

The formula row is pinned just above the stats rule, so every formula in a row sits on one line. The spare height goes between the intro and the formula. A card with no times still draws its rule, as an empty stats line, so every card ends the same way. CSS only: .rcard-formula { margin-top: auto }, and an empty .rcard-stats from the card partial (Fugu).

Build cards

A · As built

Each stack is centred in the space left between its intro and the rule (.build-card .stack { margin: auto }), so a short stack, or a two-line intro, leaves gaps above and below it, and neither tops nor bottoms line up across a row. Grilled onion burger's stack sits 57px lower than Big Boy's beside it.

B · Stacks on the base Recommended

Every stack sits on the stats rule, so the bottom buns line up across a row like burgers on a counter, and a short burger looks short beside a tall one, which is the comparison the cards are for. The spare space all goes above. CSS only: margin: auto auto 0.

C · Stacks under the intro

Each stack hangs from its intro, so the tops line up and the spare space goes below, above the rule. The bottoms don't line up, and a short burger looks like it's floating.
