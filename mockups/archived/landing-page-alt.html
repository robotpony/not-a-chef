Essays

The essay shelf is still being stocked. Four formats are planned, each anchored to a lesson rather than nostalgia for its own sake.

Food memories
Thematic vignettes from childhood or formative meals, each carrying a lesson about cooking.
Cooking reflections
What technique, ingredients, or a specific failure taught. Ratio-based thinking, post-mortems welcome.
Technique essays
A single method or principle in depth: brining, emulsification, stock. Reference material dressed as prose.
Planning essays
Meal-prep philosophy and systems for getting a weeknight dinner on the table.
