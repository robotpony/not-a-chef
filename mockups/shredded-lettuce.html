Shredded lettuce redraw

The current lettuce-shredded layer reads as tiny bananas. Each shred is short (16 to 30 units), fat (3 to 5), bent into one curve, and tapered to a point at both ends: a crescent. Shredded iceberg (Bruce's two photos, 2026-10-14) is the opposite. The strands are long, thin, and cut square. They're mostly straight or gently curled, piled into a mound, and slope down its sides from the peak, with a few strays spilling past the edge.

Each option keeps the kit's rules: one ink line, --accent-soft on the body, --surface on top faces, and texture as strokes. All of them are drawn at full size, at card size, and on a bun base, in light and dark.
