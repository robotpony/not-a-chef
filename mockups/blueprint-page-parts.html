Blueprint page: even columns, and recipes beside the parts

Two fixes to the blueprint page (partials/blueprint/page.html). The builds comparison sizes its columns by how much text each happens to have, and the parts list runs its descriptions the full page width, about 150 characters a line.

1 · Builds compared: equal columns

The table is auto-sized, so each column takes as much width as its longest text: the deluxe's long assembly line wins, the grilled onion squeezes. Proposed: table-layout: fixed, the label column at a set width, and the builds splitting the rest evenly. Every build is the same kind of thing, so each gets the same width. Also: the profile row's heavier rule now runs under its label too (it stopped short), and the empty corner says Part.

2 · Option A: a recipes sidebar beside the parts

The parts take the main column, capped at a reading width, and a sticky sidebar lists all of the blueprint's recipes as compact rows: whole burgers under their build, then recipes for a part, then the not-burgers. It uses data the site already has, apart from the middle group, which the mock sorts by hand (see §4). On a phone it falls under the parts. The card grid of recipes further down could go, since the sidebar now covers it.

3 · Option B: each part carries its recipes

No separate sidebar: each part row gets a right-hand column with the recipes that make that part. The sauces sit next to the sauce method, the buns next to assembly, the chicken and veg patties next to Not burgers. It answers "what do I actually cook for this part" in place, and the long lines shrink as a side effect. Whole-burger recipes (White Spot, McDouble) stay with their builds further up. On a phone the recipes fall under each part.

It needs one new field: part on a recipe (part: Sauces), the same field method pages already use. Seven recipes would get it.

4 · Choices

Recommendation

§1 regardless. It's a CSS change plus a colgroup.

Option B for the parts. A blueprint's point is that a burger is made of parts. Putting each recipe next to the part it makes keeps that structure, where a sidebar just lists the recipes again in another place. A is the faster win, since it needs no new data, but its middle group ("for a part") can't be sorted without the same part field anyway. Without it, A is just "No build yet · 8", which is what the page says now.

  • Recipes section at the bottom: with B it shrinks to the whole-burger recipes by build, or folds into the build cards (each already counts its recipes).
  • Not burgers: it's still an unwritten part, but it already has five recipes. B shows them anyway, which is a nudge to write the method.
  • Where it lands: templates and base CSS are Fugu's (fugu-agent); the part field on recipes, the colours, and the docs (docs/METHODS.md, FORMAT.md) are this site's.
