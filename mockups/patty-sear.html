Patty sear

The layer kit gives every top face --surface, so a patty's seared top is near-white in light mode and reads raw. B adds a third fill for seared surfaces, --sear, with its own speckle and grill-char colours per mode. C keeps the two fills and darkens the top with stipple alone; in dark mode the ink is light, so more stipple makes it lighter. D is both. Drawn from exploded-builds.html's round 4 kit, the same code export-layers.mjs ships.

Light mode
A · CurrentTop face in --surface, ink speckle. Near-white in light mode.
B · Sear tokenTop face in --sear; speckle, pits and grill char re-coloured for it.
C · Stipple onlyNo new fill: --surface top with four times the ink stipple.
D · Sear + stipple--sear top with four times the speckle, in --sear-fleck.
