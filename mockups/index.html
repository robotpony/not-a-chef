How the site was designed

Every visual change to the cookbook was mocked before it was built. These are the mocks, kept as working records: most show two or three options side by side, with the one that was picked marked. Many load the real site's pages in a frame and restyle them, so what you see is live. Back to the cookbook.

Recipe pages and cards 7

A recipe page is ingredients and method in the main column, with notes, variations and photos beside it. These shaped that page, and the card that stands for a recipe everywhere else.

  • Recipe mechanicMoving the Mechanic out of the sidebar to open the recipe as a callout.
  • Scaling calloutRedrawing the servings slider and units switch.
  • Percent scalingScaling a baker's-percentage table from what you have.
  • Sidebar photosMoving photos out of the text into the sidebar, with a viewer.
  • Recipes in developmentThe Test kitchen: publishing drafts with a banner saying they're still changing.
  • Recipe card v2The card redesign: one taxonomy row, title, intro, and a compact stat line.
  • Even cardsLining up formula rows and burger stacks across a row of cards.

A ratio stated in a recipe can be drawn as a row of small icons, and a drink as a sketch of its glass. These set the icon style and grew the kit.

Methods and blueprints 8

Methods explain how a kind of food works, so you can cook what isn't written down. A blueprint breaks a dish type, like burgers, into parts and named builds. These worked out how those pages fit together.

  • Methods pagesThe methods concept: the list, a blueprint page, its parts.
  • Methods iconA mark for methods in chips and headings.
  • Methods as a top-level sectionMethods in the menu and on the homepage.
  • Methods list with buildsWhere builds sit on the methods list.
  • Methods list balanceThe methods list on the reading layout, with a sidebar.
  • Methods list and blueprintBuilds leave the list for the blueprint page.
  • Blueprint page partsHow a blueprint page shows its parts and their recipes.
  • Blueprint lineageA timeline of the builds, placed in the sidebar.
