Green is reserved, unused. Part of the five-hue family (below) but not tokenized and has no role in the UI yet. Open question: a vegetarian/vegan indicator, or leave it out entirely. Don't invent a use for it without settling this first.

Where the palette comes from

All five hues share one saturation/lightness formula (57%/62%) at different rotations, taken directly from warpedvisions.org's shipped CSS. This site reorders the hierarchy to lead with orange→red instead of the blog's blue-led order — it does not invent new hues.

red 14°
orange 34°
yellow 54°
green 151°
blue 210°

Never introduce a hue outside this five-color family — no teal, sage, mustard, plum, ember, etc. Those were an earlier, discarded palette (see archived/landing-page-alt.html's history).

02

Type system

Three faces. No face does more than one job.

Headings & UI Libre Franklin weights 400–700
Dal Tadka
Section label · stat label · nav
Body & lists Lora shared with warpedvisions.org
Red lentils cook fast and dissolve into a creamy base. The ratio matters: 1 cup lentils to 4–5 cups water. The tadka is not garnish — it's the flavour. Don't skip it.
Quantities & data IBM Plex Mono tabular-nums, always
200g red lentils · 1.2L water · 25 min cook · 1:4–5 ratio

The mono face is load-bearing, not decorative: every quantity, time, and ratio in a recipe is set in it so numbers align visually while scanning an ingredient list mid-cook. Don't substitute another monospace family — no Fira Code, Menlo, JetBrains Mono.

Import

Put this at the top of any new mockup's <style>:

@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Libre+Franklin:ital,wght@0,400;0,500;0,600;0,700;1,500&family=Lora:ital,wght@0,400;0,500;0,600;1,400;1,500&family=IBM+Plex+Mono:wght@400;500;600&display=swap');

Line-height

Always unitless (line-height:1.6), never a fixed length (28px) — a fixed length inherits as-is regardless of a descendant's own font-size, which is what makes small text set at a large element's line-height read as disproportionately loose. Base 1.6 on body/html; tighter (1.05–1.3) for headings; 1.5–1.6 for small UI captions. List items should read like consecutive lines of surrounding prose — watch for a list item's own margin plus its wrapped paragraph's margin (a markdown "loose list" wraps each item in a <p>) stacking into oversized gaps.

Sub-head groups a long list into named sections; sub-note is a non-checkable tip nested under an ingredient — indented, smaller, no checkbox.

Mechanic callout
Red lentils cook fast and dissolve into a creamy base. The ratio matters: 1 cup lentils to 4–5 cups water.

--accent-left-bordered box for the ratio/technique explanation.

Procedure — prose (default)

Warm the oil over medium heat. Add the onions and cook, stirring occasionally, until soft and just starting to turn golden.

Stir in the garlic and spices; cook 30 seconds until fragrant, then add the lentils and water.

The default treatment — plain paragraphs. Used in most recipes; reach for the numbered variant only when steps are order-dependent.

Numbered procedure step
1Bring to high pressure, cook 45–60 minutes.
2Release pressure naturally for 20 minutes.

Mono numeral in an accent-soft square. The exception, not the default — reserved for genuinely order-dependent steps (a pressure-cooker sequence, a timed bake).
