Spreads and food colours

Two changes to the exploded build diagrams (mockups/exploded-builds.html). First, the mayo: knife marks that read as a spread, and where the spread sits, so a build like the Whopper stops spending two layers on it. Second, three soft food colours for lettuce and pickles, tomato and ketchup, and mustard, as kit-only tokens like --sear. The drawings are the real round 4 kit; only the spreads are new.

1 · Texture: the knife's path on the top face

From Bruce's toast photo: broad overlapping S-shaped swoops, each ending in a raised lip where the knife lifted, with a smooth surface between. Today's three fine arcs read as lines drawn on a disc, not as something spread. Three ways to draw it, switched for the whole page from the topbar. Swoops: long S-curves, each ending in a tapered ink lip with a faint echo below it (the shadow under the lip). Scallops: the same lips on short arcs, the way a knife leaves them when it sweeps round. Lines: the swoop's lip as a pair of fine lines that meet at the ends, lighter, closer to the hatching elsewhere in the kit.

2 · Placement: the spread on the bun

Mayo nearly always goes straight onto the bun: under the top bun or on the base (the Whopper, Bruce burger, drive-in deluxe, pub burger, Teen sauce; burger sauce too). The diagram looks down from about 20° above, so the base's cut face is in view and the top bun's isn't. Each option is the Whopper's real stack.

Heights are the diagram's height at full size, labels included. Card size (150px) is below each.

3 · Food colours

Each colour is a pair, as the kit's fills are now: a deeper side (bodies, and the sauce line inside a swirl's ink) and a paler top. Lettuce and pickles are green, tomato and ketchup red, mustard yellow. Everything else (bun, onion, cheese, mayo, patty) keeps the kit's two fills and the sear. This is Fuller (2026-10-14: Soft was too close to the bun at card size, and a truer lettuce green would break STYLE.md's five-hue rule). The placement stacks above use it too.

The coloured layers, light and dark

Shredded lettuce in colour

The strands are about 2px wide, so the outline is most of what you see. With a green line and a dark fill (first), dark mode shows hollow tubes: pale edges around nothing. Drawn as solid green ribbons with no outline (second), each strand is one mark of colour in both themes, the way the char lines on a grilled patty are solid ink. The third keeps a faint ink hairline around the solid ribbon, for separation where strands cross.

4 · Recommendation

Texture: Swoops. It's the toast photo's move (a long sweep that ends in a ridge where the knife lifted), it still reads at card size, and the tapered ink stroke matches the char lines on grilled patties. Scallops read as fish scales or stacked coins, and Lines gets lost at 150px.

Placement: C, or B if the opened lid doesn't read. The base carrying its own spread (B) is simply true: the cut face is in view, the spread sits on it with a rim of crumb around it like the toast, and the separate layer goes away. The top bun can't show its underside from this angle, so the choice there is a cheat either way. C opens the lid toward you, the way burger photos are shot, and is the most honest picture of where the mayo is; it also saves the most height. D (your option 3) is a bun-wide disc seen from below, which shows the knife marks on the right face but asks the reader to switch viewpoints mid-stack.

Colour: Fuller, in-family. Soft is too close to the bun's fill to read at card size. The mint cast of 151° green is noticeable on the leaf but fine on shredded lettuce and pickles, so it doesn't justify breaking the five-hue rule. Shredded lettuce is the one exception to "one ink line": its strands are about 2px wide, too thin for a fill inside an outline, so each is a solid green ribbon with no outline (--kit-green-ink, a mid green in both themes). In dark mode every colour drops to a dark tint, so nothing glows (round 1's full colour turned patties pink in dark mode; these leave the patty alone).

Syntax, if B or C lands: a modifier rather than new keys, so any spread can sit on any bun: bun-base + mayo, bun-top + sauce-burger. The build pages' stack blocks would then change (a content-agent edit), and Fugu's render hook learns the + (a fugu-agent handoff).
