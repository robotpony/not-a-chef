3

Archive stats display: ✅ plain stat rail kept — already proven in two contexts; ledger discarded.

4

Where search & theme-toggle live: ✅ homepage's answer won — hero search, footer toggle.

5

Page-level note: ✅ inline caveat won, renamed .section-caveat.

6

Essay-format and reference-guide teasers: ✅ card grid and .browse-list won.

Promote the unnamed-but-real components: ✅ all promoted into STYLE.md and demonstrated in style.html §3.
