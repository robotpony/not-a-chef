Food Log months

The Food Log sidebar lists every month: 39 rows, and the 20 before mid-2025 say "undated" in mono, because those months were written as lists without a heading per day. These are real Food Log pages with the Months list rebuilt per option. The sidebar's length is measured live.

The options

A · As built

One flat list, newest first. Every row carries its year in the label, and a month without day headings says "undated" where the day count goes.

B · By year

The same rows under a small year label, so each label drops its year ("October", "Fall"). The count shows only when there are days to count; an undated month just has no number. The list is still all 39 rows.

C · Older years folded Recommended

B, with only the newest year open, and the year of the page you're on. Every other year is one row ("2024 · 4 months") that opens in place. That's 16 rows on this month's page in place of 39, and nothing to hide or link elsewhere.

Questions

  • A, B, or C?
  • In B and C, do season and multi-month labels keep their full name ("Fall and winter 2022–23"), as shown, while plain months drop the year?
  • In C, is a folded year's count in months right, or would a day count read better (only the recent years have one)?
