A glossary of concepts, linked from the text

Bruce writes the definitions, one short file per concept. A tool finds words worth defining and turns the first mention on each page into an ordinary wiki link, after showing the change. Hugo does the rest at build: the glossary page, a glossary.json, and a quiet hover on every linked word. No marking script runs in the browser.

The examples are real paragraphs from the cookbook. The definitions are stand-ins for Bruce's own.

02

On a phone

No hover on a phone, so a tap on the word goes to its glossary entry, as a [[Term]] glossary link does today. The entry is highlighted where it lands, and Back returns to the same place in the recipe.

Recipe · Pan sauces

A good fond gives you most of a good sauce, and no amount of butter rescues a pan with none. Pour off all but a tablespoon of fat, add the wine, and scrape as it bubbles: that is the deglaze, and it takes about a minute.

After the tap · Glossary
Deglaze also deglazing, deglazed

Pour a liquid into a hot pan and scrape, so the browned bits stuck to the bottom dissolve into it. Wine, stock, beer, or water all work; the liquid only has to be cold enough to lift the fond and hot enough to keep bubbling.

03

The glossary page

A to Z, under Reference, one <dl> per letter. Each entry is a <dt> with the term in a <dfn> and an id to link to, then its definition. Hugo adds the other forms and the pages that link to it. A term with its own essay (Win the fridge) is listed too, with its description and a link to the essay, so every concept is findable in one place.

Instead, I use a variant of [[Reverse cooking: reverse sear and water frying|water frying]] that works well with ground meat. … If anything does stick, add a splash of water (or wine) and [[Deglaze|deglaze]].

The marking is a plain wiki link, so Obsidian shows and follows it too, and the render hook can't tell a tool's link from one typed by hand. When the word matches the title exactly, the link is just [[Fond]].

The HTML Hugo writes

Standard elements only. The word is a link; the definition is its description; the glossary is a definition list.

In a page (render-link.html)
<span class="pop-host">
  <a class="glossary-link" href="/reference/glossary/#deglaze"
     aria-describedby="g-deglaze-1">deglaze</a>
  <span class="pop pop-up" id="g-deglaze-1" role="tooltip">
    <span class="pop-head">Deglaze</span>
    <span>Pour a liquid into a hot pan and scrape, …</span>
  </span>
</span>
On /reference/glossary/
<dl class="glossary">
  <dt id="deglaze"><dfn>Deglaze</dfn>
    <span class="also">also <i>deglazing</i>, …</span></dt>
  <dd><p>Pour a liquid into a hot pan …</p>
    <p class="used">Used in <a href="…">Pan sauces</a>, …</p></dd>
</dl>

/reference/glossary/index.json

  "def":"Pour a liquid into a hot pan and scrape, …",

Glossary entries don't get pages of their own: each file builds into the list only. The JSON is for other tools and sites (w42, warped.js), not for this page.
