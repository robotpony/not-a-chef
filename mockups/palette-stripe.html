Palette stripe

A palette stripe along the top of the page and above the footer. The layouts come from warpedvisions.org (A) and Space Command (B); the colours are this site's own tokens: the accent and its rust, the cuisine ochre, the principle green, and the draft slate. The five base hues in STYLE.md are warpedvisions' colours, so they aren't used here. Scroll inside each frame: A's bars are fixed, the others scroll away with the header.

The proposal (C)

  • Colours: the site's tokens, not the base hues: --accent as the long field, then --accent-strong (rust), --highlight (ochre), --principle (green), and --flag (slate) outermost, mirrored at both ends. The stripe is the same colours a reader already sees in links, chips, and the test kitchen strip, and dark mode follows the tokens with no extra rule.
  • Where: Space Command's placement: a 6px band as the first thing in the header (.site-nav::before, no new markup), and a 3px band with rounded ends across the footer's content column, above its text (.site-footer-inner::before). Both scroll with the page, as the header already does.
  • Size: 24px blocks at the top, 12px in the footer band; at phone width the top band drops to 5px with 16px blocks. Slimmer than both sources, to match the softened scheme.
  • Why not fixed: warpedvisions' fixed bars sit over the page at z-index 999 and need JS. This is a book; a band that stays on screen while you read a recipe is chrome, not a signature.
  • Palette rule: decoration only, the one place colour carries no role, as on Space Command; no colour outside the tokens.
